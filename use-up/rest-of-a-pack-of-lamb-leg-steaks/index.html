What to cook with the rest of a pack of lamb leg steaks

Lamb leg steak usually comes as a pack of 2. A dinner for one tends to use about 180 g, which leaves around 120 g behind. All 8 recipes here use it, so put two or three in the same week and the pack gets finished.

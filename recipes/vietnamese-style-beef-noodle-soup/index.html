Vietnamese-Style Beef Noodle Soup

A single portion of Vietnamese cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 70grice noodles
  • 300mlbeef stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1star anise
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Soak the rice noodles in boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2

    Simmer the stock with the star anise for 5 minutes, then remove the star anise.

  3. 3

    Add the beef slices to the hot stock and poach for 1–2 minutes until just cooked.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the noodles in the broth with the beef, spring onion and coriander.

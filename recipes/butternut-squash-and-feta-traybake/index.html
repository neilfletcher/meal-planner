Butternut Squash & Feta Traybake

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 33 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
25 min
Total
33 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 200gbutternut squash, cubed
  • ½red onion, cut into wedges
  • small handfulfresh parsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 50gfeta cheese
Store cupboard
  • 1 ½ tbspolive oil
  • 50gcouscous
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • pinchchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Toss the squash and red onion wedges with the olive oil, cumin and chilli flakes on a baking tray.

  3. 3

    Roast for 20 minutes, then crumble the feta over the top and roast for a further 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    While the vegetables finish roasting, cover the couscous with boiling water and leave for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the roasted squash and feta over the couscous, scattered with chopped parsley.

Australian-Style Lamb Chops with Mint

A single portion of Australian cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
12 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2lamb chops
Fresh produce
  • handfulfresh mint, chopped
  • 120gnew potatoes
  • 60ggreen beans
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspnatural yoghurt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes and green beans together for 10–12 minutes until tender, then drain.

  2. 2

    Fry the lamb chops over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes each side until browned and cooked to your liking.

  3. 3

    Stir the mint through the yoghurt.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the chops with the potatoes, beans and minted yoghurt.

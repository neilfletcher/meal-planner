Butter Chicken with Basmati Rice

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken breast, diced
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsingle cream or plain yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspbutter chicken or tikka curry paste
  • 100mlpassata
  • 70gbasmati rice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the curry paste and crushed garlic in a splash of oil for 1 minute until fragrant.

  3. 3

    Add the chicken and cook for 3–4 minutes until sealed.

  4. 4

    Stir in the passata and simmer for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  5. 5

    Stir through the cream, taste and adjust seasoning, then serve over the rice scattered with coriander.

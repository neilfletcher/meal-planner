Sri Lankan-Style Coconut Dhal

A single portion of Sri Lankan cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve. It brings some heat.

Prep
6 min
Cook
15 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
VeganSpicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 80gdried red lentils
  • 150mlcoconut milk
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground turmeric
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Rinse the lentils, then simmer with the turmeric, chilli flakes, garlic and 200ml water for 12 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Stir in the coconut milk and simmer for 3 minutes until thickened.

  3. 3

    Season and serve with the flatbread.

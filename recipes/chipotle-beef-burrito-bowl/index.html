Chipotle Beef Burrito Bowl

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef mince or steak strips
Fresh produce
  • ½avocado, sliced
Frozen
  • 60gsweetcorn
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspchipotle paste
  • 150gcooked rice
  • 100gtinned black beans, drained
  • 3 tbspshop-bought salsa
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the beef in a hot pan for 4–5 minutes until browned, stirring through the chipotle paste for the final minute.

  2. 2

    Warm the black beans and sweetcorn together in a small pan.

  3. 3

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  4. 4

    Build the bowl with the rice, beans and sweetcorn, beef, salsa and sliced avocado.

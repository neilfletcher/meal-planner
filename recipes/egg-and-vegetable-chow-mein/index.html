Egg & Vegetable Chow Mein

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 100gstir-fry vegetable mix
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 2eggs
Store cupboard
  • 100gdried egg noodles
  • 1 ½ tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tspsesame oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions, then drain.

  2. 2

    Beat the eggs and scramble in a wok or frying pan over medium heat until just set, then set aside.

  3. 3

    Stir-fry the vegetables and garlic for 3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the noodles and soy sauce, and toss for 1–2 minutes until hot through.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Fold the egg back through and drizzle with sesame oil to serve.

Mexican-Style Turkey & Black Bean Chilli

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
14 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gturkey mince
Store cupboard
  • 1 tinblack beans, drained
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 70grice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the turkey mince in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 5 minutes, breaking it up, until browned.

  3. 3

    Stir in the paprika and chilli flakes, then add the black beans and chopped tomatoes.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Simmer for 8 minutes and serve over the rice.

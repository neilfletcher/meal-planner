Canadian-Style Maple Pork Chops

A single portion of Canadian cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
10 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2pork chops
Fresh produce
  • 120gnew potatoes
  • 60ggreen beans
Store cupboard
  • 1 ½ tbspmaple syrup
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes and green beans together for 12 minutes until tender, then drain.

  2. 2

    Fry the pork chops over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes each side until nearly cooked through.

  3. 3

    Stir the maple syrup and mustard together, pour over the chops and cook for 1–2 minutes until glazed.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve with the potatoes and beans.

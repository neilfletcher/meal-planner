Chilean-Style Pork Chop with Pebre Salsa

A single portion of Chilean cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1pork loin chop
Fresh produce
  • 1tomato, finely diced
  • ¼red onion, finely diced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • ½red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the tomato, red onion, garlic, chilli and coriander with the red wine vinegar and olive oil to make the pebre salsa. Season and set aside.

  2. 2

    Season the pork chop and pan-fry in the vegetable oil over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes per side until cooked through and golden.

  3. 3

    Rest the pork chop for a couple of minutes.

  4. 4

    Serve topped with a generous spoonful of pebre salsa.

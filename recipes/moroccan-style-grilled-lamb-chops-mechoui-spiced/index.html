Moroccan-Style Grilled Lamb Chops (Mechoui-Spiced)

A single portion of Moroccan cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2lamb chops (about 200g)
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1lemon, juiced
  • 1tomato, sliced
  • ¼cucumber, sliced
  • ¼red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbspmint, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • 1 tsppaprika
  • ½ tspground coriander
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the garlic, cumin, paprika, coriander, oil and lemon juice into a paste and rub over the lamb chops. Leave to marinate for 5 minutes if time allows.

  2. 2

    Heat a griddle or frying pan over high heat and cook the chops for 3–4 minutes each side, until charred and cooked to your liking.

  3. 3

    Rest for 2 minutes while you warm the flatbread.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Toss the tomato, cucumber, red onion and mint together and serve alongside the chops with the flatbread.

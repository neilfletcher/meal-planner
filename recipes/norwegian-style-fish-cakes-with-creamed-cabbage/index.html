Norwegian-Style Fish Cakes with Creamed Cabbage

A single portion of Norwegian cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gwhite fish fillet (e.g. haddock or cod), roughly chopped
Fresh produce
  • 100gwhite cabbage, shredded
  • 1 tbspfresh dill, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 2 tbspmilk
  • 2 tbspbutter
  • 2 tbspdouble cream
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspplain flour
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchnutmeg
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Blitz or finely chop the fish, then mix with the flour, egg, milk and nutmeg to make a smooth, spoonable mixture.

  2. 2

    Heat half the butter in a pan and fry spoonfuls of the fish mixture for 3–4 minutes each side until golden and cooked through.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile melt the remaining butter in another pan and soften the cabbage for 6–8 minutes until tender.

  4. 4

    Stir the cream into the cabbage and simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the fish cakes with the creamed cabbage, scattered with dill.

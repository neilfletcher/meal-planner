Nigerian-Style Efo Riro Spinach & Turkey Stew

A single portion of Nigerian cooking, ready in 29 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
20 min
Total
29 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gturkey breast, diced
Fresh produce
  • 200gspinach, roughly chopped
  • ½onion, chopped
  • ½red pepper, chopped
  • ½scotch bonnet chilli, finely chopped
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
Store cupboard
  • 200gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspred palm oil (or vegetable oil)
  • 1 tspvegetable stock powder
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Finely chop the onion, red pepper and chilli together (or blitz in a food processor) to form a base paste.

  2. 2

    Heat the palm oil in a saucepan over medium heat, add the pepper paste, garlic and ginger, and cook for 5–6 minutes until softened and fragrant.

  3. 3

    Add the chopped tomatoes and stock powder and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the turkey and cook for 8–10 minutes until cooked through.

  5. 5

    Stir in the spinach and cook for a further 2–3 minutes until just wilted.

  6. 6

    Season to taste and serve.

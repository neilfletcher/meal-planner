Australian-Style Beef and Beetroot Burger

A single portion of Australian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • 1lettuce leaf
  • 1slice tomato
Dairy and eggs
  • 1slice cheese
Store cupboard
  • 1slice cooked beetroot
  • 1 tspmayonnaise
  • 1 tsptomato ketchup
  • ½ tspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsalt and pepper
Bakery
  • 1burger bun
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Shape the beef mince into a patty and season well.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the patty for 4–5 minutes each side until cooked through, topping with the cheese slice for the last minute to melt.

  3. 3

    Toast the burger bun cut-side down in the same pan for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Spread the bun with mayonnaise and ketchup.

  5. 5

    Build the burger with the lettuce, the patty and cheese, and a slice of beetroot and tomato.

Indonesian-Style Soto Ayam (Turmeric Chicken Soup with Noodles and Egg)

A single portion of Indonesian cooking, ready in 29 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
20 min
Total
29 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken thigh fillets
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1lemongrass stalk, bruised
  • 40gbeansprouts
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1lime wedge
Dairy and eggs
  • 1hard-boiled egg, halved
Store cupboard
  • 400mlchicken stock
  • 50gvermicelli noodles
  • 1 tspchilli sauce or sambal
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground turmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the egg for 8 minutes, then cool and peel. Put the chicken, stock, turmeric, garlic, ginger and lemongrass into a saucepan, bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  2. 2

    Remove the chicken, shred it, and return it to the pan.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, soak the noodles in boiled water for 4–5 minutes until tender, then drain and place in a bowl.

  4. 4

    Ladle the hot soup and shredded chicken over the noodles.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Top with the boiled egg, beansprouts and spring onion, and serve with the lime wedge and chilli sauce on the side.

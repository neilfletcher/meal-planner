Trinidadian-Style Fried Fish Bake with Tamarind Sauce

A single portion of Trinidadian cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gfirm white fish fillet (e.g. pollock or coley)
Fresh produce
  • 1 handfullettuce, shredded
  • 1tomato, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 60gself-raising flour
  • ¼ tspbaking powder
  • 80mlwater
  • 3 tbspvegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tbsptamarind sauce or chutney
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchsalt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and water into a smooth batter for the fried bake, and let it rest for 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Fry spoonfuls of the batter in the hot oil for 2–3 minutes each side until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper.

  3. 3

    Season the fish and shallow-fry in a little oil for 3–4 minutes each side until golden and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Split the bakes open and fill with the fried fish, lettuce and tomato.

  5. 5

    Drizzle with the tamarind sauce to serve.

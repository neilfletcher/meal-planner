Israeli-Style Shakshuka

A single portion of Israeli cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve. It brings some heat.

Prep
6 min
Cook
12 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
VegSpicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • ½onion, diced
Dairy and eggs
  • 2eggs
Store cupboard
  • 200gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the onion and red pepper in a splash of oil over medium heat for 5 minutes until softening.

  2. 2

    Stir in the cumin and chilli flakes, then add the chopped tomatoes and simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.

  3. 3

    Make two wells in the sauce, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 4–5 minutes until the whites are set.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve straight from the pan with the flatbread.

Chilean-Style Porotos Granados Bean & Squash Stew

A single portion of Chilean cooking, ready in 29 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
20 min
Total
29 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 150gbutternut squash, diced
  • ½onion, diced
  • ½red pepper, diced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 handfulfresh basil, torn
Frozen
  • 80gsweetcorn kernels
Store cupboard
  • 400gborlotti or cannellini beans (tinned), drained
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 200mlvegetable stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the olive oil in a saucepan and sauté the onion, pepper and garlic for 4–5 minutes until softened.

  2. 2

    Add the squash, paprika and stock, bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 10–12 minutes until the squash is tender.

  3. 3

    Stir in the beans and sweetcorn and simmer for a further 5 minutes until heated through and the stew has thickened slightly.

  4. 4

    Season to taste and scatter with torn basil to serve.

Armenian-Style Stuffed Pepper Bowl

A single portion of Armenian cooking, ready in 44 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
35 min
Total
44 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • 1red pepper, halved and deseeded
Store cupboard
  • 50grice
  • 100gchopped tomatoes
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Part-cook the rice for 8 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, fry the beef mince with the cinnamon in a splash of oil for 5 minutes until browned, then mix with the rice and pile into the pepper halves.

  3. 3

    Place in a small ovenproof dish, spoon over the chopped tomatoes, cover with foil.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Bake at 190°C (fan 170°C) for 25 minutes until the pepper is tender.

Sumac Turkey Flatbread with Pickled Onion

A single portion of Turkish cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gturkey breast steak, sliced
Fresh produce
  • ¼red onion, thinly sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 3 tbspshop-bought hummus
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsumac
    Can't find it?Use finely grated lemon zest with a pinch of salt. The planner can swap it on your shopping list for you.
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the red onion with the vinegar and set aside to quick-pickle while you cook.

  2. 2

    Toss the turkey with the sumac and olive oil.

  3. 3

    Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the turkey for 3–4 minutes each side until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Warm the flatbread and spread with the hummus.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then top with the sliced turkey and drained pickled onion.

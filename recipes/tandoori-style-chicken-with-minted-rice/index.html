Tandoori-Style Chicken with Minted Rice

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken breast or thigh, sliced
Fresh produce
  • small handfulfresh mint, chopped
  • ½lemon
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspplain yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbsptandoori curry paste
  • 70gbasmati rice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions, then stir through the chopped mint.

  2. 2

    Mix the tandoori paste with the yoghurt and coat the chicken.

  3. 3

    Heat a griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the chicken for 5–6 minutes, turning occasionally, until charred and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the minted rice with a squeeze of lemon.

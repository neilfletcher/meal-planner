Ethiopian-Inspired Spiced Red Lentil Stew

A single portion of Ethiopian cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 80gdried red lentils
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • ½ tspground ginger
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Rinse the lentils well.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion and garlic in a splash of oil for 3–4 minutes until softening.

  3. 3

    Stir in the paprika, ginger, cinnamon and chilli flakes, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then add the lentils, chopped tomatoes and 300ml water, and simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are tender and the stew has thickened.

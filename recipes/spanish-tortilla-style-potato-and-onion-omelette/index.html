Spanish Tortilla-Style Potato & Onion Omelette

A single portion of Spanish cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 150gpotato, thinly sliced
  • ½onion, thinly sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 3eggs
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a small non-stick frying pan and gently fry the potato and onion for 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until tender.

  2. 2

    Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pour over the potatoes, pressing them down evenly.

  3. 3

    Cook over low heat for 4–5 minutes until mostly set.

  4. 4

    Place a plate over the pan, flip the tortilla out, then slide it back in to cook for a further 2 minutes on the other side.

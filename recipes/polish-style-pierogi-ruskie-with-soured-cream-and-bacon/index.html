Polish-Style Pierogi Ruskie with Soured Cream and Bacon

A single portion of Polish cooking, ready in 35 minutes with 10 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2bacon rashers, chopped
Fresh produce
  • 150gpotato, peeled and chopped
  • ¼onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbspchives, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 50gcurd cheese or cream cheese
  • 15gbutter
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 10fresh dumpling or wonton wrappers
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potato in salted water for 12–15 minutes until tender, then drain, mash and leave to cool slightly.

  2. 2

    Mix the mashed potato with the curd cheese and a little of the chopped onion, and season well.

  3. 3

    Place a spoonful of the potato mixture in the centre of each wrapper, dampen the edges and fold into half-moons, pressing to seal.

  4. 4

    Cook the pierogi in a pan of simmering water for 3–4 minutes until they float, then drain.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile, fry the bacon and remaining onion in the butter until crisp and golden.

  6. 6

    Toss the drained pierogi through the bacon and butter, and serve with a dollop of soured cream and a scattering of chives.

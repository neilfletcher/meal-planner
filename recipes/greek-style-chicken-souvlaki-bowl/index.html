Greek-Style Chicken Souvlaki Bowl

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
9 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken breast, cubed
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
  • 60gcucumber, diced
Store cupboard
  • 70grice
  • 2 tbspshop-bought tzatziki
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried oregano
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Toss the chicken with the oregano, a squeeze of lemon and a splash of oil.

  3. 3

    Fry over medium-high heat for 7–8 minutes until browned and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the rice with the cucumber and a dollop of tzatziki.

Norwegian-Style Creamy Fish Soup (Fiskesuppe)

A single portion of Norwegian cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gskinless cod or haddock fillet, cut into chunks
Fresh produce
  • 1small carrot, diced
  • ½leek, sliced
  • 1small potato, diced
  • 1 tbspfresh dill, chopped
  • 1lemon wedge
Dairy and eggs
  • 100mldouble cream
  • 15gbutter
Store cupboard
  • 400mlfish or vegetable stock
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a saucepan, add the carrot, leek and potato, and cook gently for 5 minutes until starting to soften.

  2. 2

    Pour in the stock, bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes until the vegetables are tender.

  3. 3

    Stir in the cream and bring back to a gentle simmer, then add the fish and poach for 4–5 minutes until just cooked through.

  4. 4

    Stir through most of the dill and season to taste.

  5. 5

    Ladle into a bowl, scatter with the remaining dill, and serve with the lemon wedge.

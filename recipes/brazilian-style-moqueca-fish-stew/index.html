Brazilian-Style Moqueca Fish Stew

A single portion of Brazilian cooking, ready in 27 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
18 min
Total
27 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gwhite fish fillet, cubed
Fresh produce
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
  • 1lime, juiced
Store cupboard
  • 150mlcoconut milk
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Marinate the fish briefly in half the lime juice and a pinch of salt.

  2. 2

    Soften the onion, garlic and pepper in the oil for 4–5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the tomato, chilli flakes and coconut milk, and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the fish and simmer gently for 8–10 minutes until just cooked through.

  5. 5

    Stir through the coriander and remaining lime juice, and serve with rice.

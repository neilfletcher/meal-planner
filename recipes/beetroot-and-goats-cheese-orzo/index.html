Beetroot & Goat's Cheese Orzo

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 100gcooked beetroot (vacuum-packed)
  • handfulbaby spinach
Dairy and eggs
  • 50ggoat's cheese
Store cupboard
  • 70gorzo pasta
  • 1 tbspwalnuts
  • 1 tspbalsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the orzo in salted boiling water according to the packet instructions, then drain.

  2. 2

    Dice the beetroot and roughly chop the walnuts.

  3. 3

    Toss the warm orzo with the olive oil and balsamic vinegar, then stir through the spinach so it wilts slightly in the heat.

  4. 4

    Fold in the diced beetroot and crumble over the goat's cheese.

  5. 5

    Scatter with the chopped walnuts and serve.

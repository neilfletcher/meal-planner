Moroccan Harira-Style Lentil & Chickpea Soup

A single portion of Moroccan cooking, ready in 30 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
22 min
Total
30 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1celery stick, diced
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
  • 1lemon wedge
Store cupboard
  • 40gred lentils
  • 100gtinned chickpeas, drained
  • 200gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 300mlvegetable stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground ginger
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
  • ½ tspground turmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Soften the onion and celery in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4–5 minutes, then add the garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute.

  2. 2

    Add the lentils, chickpeas, tomatoes and stock. Bring to a simmer.

  3. 3

    Cook for 18–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are soft and the soup has thickened.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Stir through the coriander and a squeeze of lemon before serving.

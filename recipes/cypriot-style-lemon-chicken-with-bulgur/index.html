Cypriot-Style Lemon Chicken with Bulgur

A single portion of Cypriot cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 60gbulgur wheat
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried oregano
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the bulgur wheat according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the chicken and garlic in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 8 minutes until browned and cooked through.

  3. 3

    Stir in the oregano and a squeeze of lemon.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the chicken over the bulgur.

German-Style Currywurst with Fries

A single portion of German cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
6 min
Cook
20 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2pork sausages, sliced
Frozen
  • 150gfrozen fries
Store cupboard
  • 3 tbspketchup
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspcurry powder
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the fries according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the sliced sausages in a pan over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes until browned all over.

  3. 3

    Warm the ketchup with the curry powder and smoked paprika in a small pan.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Spoon the curry sauce over the sausages and serve with the fries.

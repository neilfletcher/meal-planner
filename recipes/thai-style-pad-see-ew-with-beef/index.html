Thai-Style Pad See Ew with Beef

A single portion of Thai cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef rump or sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
  • 60gtenderstem broccoli, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 150gflat rice noodles (fresh or soaked)
  • 1 tbspdark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsplight soy sauce
  • 1 tspsugar
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the beef and garlic for 2–3 minutes until browned. Push to one side.

  2. 2

    Crack in the egg and scramble briefly, then stir through the beef.

  3. 3

    Add the noodles, broccoli, soy sauces and sugar. Toss over high heat for 3–4 minutes, letting the noodles catch slightly, until everything is well combined.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve immediately.

Belgian-Style Stoemp with Pork Sausage

A single portion of Belgian cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
18 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gpork sausages
Fresh produce
  • 200gpotatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 80gcurly kale, shredded
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspbutter
  • 2 tbspmilk
Store cupboard
  • ½ tspgrain mustard
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchsalt and pepper
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Grill or fry the sausages for 12–15 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and cooked through.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile boil the potatoes for 12–15 minutes until tender.

  3. 3

    Add the kale to the potato pan for the last 3–4 minutes of cooking to wilt and soften.

  4. 4

    Drain, then mash the potatoes and kale together with the butter, milk and mustard until roughly mashed, and season with salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    Serve the stoemp topped with the sausages.

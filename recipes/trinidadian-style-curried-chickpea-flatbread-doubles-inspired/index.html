Trinidadian-Style Curried Chickpea Flatbread (Doubles-Inspired)

A single portion of Trinidadian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
VeganSpicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 60gcucumber, diced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tinchickpeas, drained
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspcurry powder
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Warm the chickpeas with the curry powder and chilli flakes in a splash of oil for 8 minutes, mashing some as you go.

  2. 2

    Warm the flatbread.

  3. 3

    Pile the spiced chickpeas onto the flatbread.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Top with the diced cucumber to serve.

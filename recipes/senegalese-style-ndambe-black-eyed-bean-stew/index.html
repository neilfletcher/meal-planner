Senegalese-Style Ndambe Black-Eyed Bean Stew

A single portion of Senegalese cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
7 min
Cook
16 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1lime, juiced
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 240gtinned black-eyed beans, drained
  • 1 tbsptomato puree
  • 100mlvegetable stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground ginger
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat a splash of oil in a pan and fry the onion and garlic for 2–3 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Stir in the tomato, tomato puree, ginger and chilli flakes, and cook for 2 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the black-eyed beans and stock, and simmer for 12–15 minutes until thickened.

  4. 4

    Squeeze in the lime juice and season to taste. Scatter with coriander to serve.

Lebanese-Style Freekeh Pilaf with Spiced Chicken

A single portion of Lebanese cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken thigh fillets, diced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
  • 1lemon wedge
Store cupboard
  • 75gcracked freekeh
  • 1 tbsptoasted flaked almonds
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 200mlchicken stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
  • ½ tspground allspice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Rinse the freekeh. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the onion for 2–3 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Add the diced chicken and spices, and cook for 3–4 minutes until the chicken starts to colour.

  3. 3

    Stir in the freekeh and stock, bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 15–18 minutes until the freekeh is tender and the liquid absorbed.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then fluff through with a fork, scatter with the toasted almonds and parsley, and finish with a squeeze of lemon.

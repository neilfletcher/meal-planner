Persian-Style Ghormeh Sabzi Herb & Lamb Stew

A single portion of Persian cooking, ready in 30 minutes with 10 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120glamb leg steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • 20gparsley, chopped
  • 20gcoriander, chopped
  • 1spring onion, chopped
  • 50gspinach, chopped
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsplemon juice
Store cupboard
  • 100gtinned red kidney beans, drained
  • 200mlstock
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 70gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspturmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan over high heat, add the lamb and turmeric and sear for 2–3 minutes until browned but still pink inside. Tip onto a plate.

  3. 3

    Fry the onion until soft, then add the herbs and spinach and fry for 3–4 minutes until darkened and fragrant.

  4. 4

    Add the stock, lemon juice and kidney beans and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.

  5. 5

    Return the lamb and its juices for 2 minutes. Season to taste and serve with rice.

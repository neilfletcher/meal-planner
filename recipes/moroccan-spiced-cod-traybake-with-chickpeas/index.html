Moroccan-Spiced Cod Traybake with Chickpeas

A single portion of Moroccan cooking, ready in 25 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
18 min
Total
25 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gcod fillet
Fresh produce
  • 100gcherry tomatoes
  • ½lemon
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 200gtinned chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspras el hanout
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Toss the chickpeas and cherry tomatoes with half the oil and the ras el hanout on a baking tray, and roast for 8 minutes.

  3. 3

    Rub the cod with the remaining oil, nestle it on top of the chickpeas, and squeeze over the lemon juice.

  4. 4

    Roast for a further 10 minutes until the cod flakes easily.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Scatter with coriander to serve.

Cullen Skink-Style Smoked Haddock Chowder

A single portion of British cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsmoked haddock fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1potato, diced
  • 1small onion, chopped
  • 1 tbspchives, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 15gbutter
  • 100mlmilk
Store cupboard
  • 100mlfish or vegetable stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1bay leaf
  • to tasteblack pepper
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a saucepan and soften the onion for 3–4 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add the potato, stock and bay leaf, cover and simmer for 10 minutes until the potato is tender.

  3. 3

    Pour in the milk and lay the haddock on top, then poach gently for 5–6 minutes until the fish flakes easily.

  4. 4

    Lift out the fish, remove the skin and flake it back into the soup, lightly crushing some potato as you stir.

  5. 5

    Season with black pepper and scatter with chives to serve.

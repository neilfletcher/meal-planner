New Zealand-Style Lamb with Mint Sauce

A single portion of New Zealand cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
10 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160glamb leg steak
Fresh produce
  • handfulfresh mint, chopped
  • 120gnew potatoes
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 1 tsphoney
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the new potatoes for 12 minutes until tender, then drain.

  2. 2

    Mix the mint, vinegar and honey into a sauce.

  3. 3

    Fry the lamb over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes each side, then rest for 2 minutes and slice.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the lamb with the potatoes and mint sauce.

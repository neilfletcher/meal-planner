Dutch-Style Pea Soup with Sausage

A single portion of Dutch cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
13 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1smoked sausage, sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
Frozen
  • 150gfrozen peas
Store cupboard
  • 250mlvegetable stock
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the onion in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Add the stock and peas, and simmer for 6 minutes.

  3. 3

    Blitz or mash roughly, then stir in the sausage slices and warm through for 2 minutes.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve with the crusty bread.

Black Bean & Sweetcorn Quesadillas

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
8 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Dairy and eggs
  • 50gcheddar, grated
Frozen
  • 60gsweetcorn
Store cupboard
  • 100gtinned black beans, drained
  • 3 tbspshop-bought salsa
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Bakery
  • 2flour tortillas
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Roughly mash the black beans with the sweetcorn and smoked paprika.

  2. 2

    Spread over one tortilla, scatter with cheese, and top with the second tortilla.

  3. 3

    Dry-fry in a large pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes each side until golden and the cheese has melted.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Cut into wedges and serve with the salsa.

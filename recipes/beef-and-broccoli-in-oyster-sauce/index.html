Beef & Broccoli in Oyster Sauce

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 130gbeef frying strips
Fresh produce
  • 120gtenderstem broccoli
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspoyster sauce
  • 1 tspsoy sauce
  • 70gjasmine rice
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Chop the broccoli into bite-sized pieces and finely chop the garlic.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the beef for 1–2 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

  4. 4

    Add the broccoli to the wok with a splash of water, cover and steam-fry for 2–3 minutes until just tender.

  5. 5

    Return the beef to the wok with the garlic, oyster sauce and soy sauce, and toss for 1 minute until glossy.

  6. 6

    Season to taste, then serve over the rice.

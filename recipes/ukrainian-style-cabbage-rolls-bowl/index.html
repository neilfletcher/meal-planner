Ukrainian-Style Cabbage Rolls Bowl

A single portion of Ukrainian cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gpork mince
Fresh produce
  • 150gcabbage, shredded
  • ½onion, diced
Store cupboard
  • 50grice
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Part-cook the rice for 8 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion and pork mince in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the cabbage and rice, and cook for 4 minutes until the cabbage softens.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Stir in the chopped tomatoes and simmer for 5 minutes before serving.

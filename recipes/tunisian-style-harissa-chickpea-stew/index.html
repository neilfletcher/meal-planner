Tunisian-Style Harissa Chickpea Stew

A single portion of Tunisian cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve. It brings some heat.

Prep
7 min
Cook
12 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
VeganSpicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tinchickpeas, drained
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspharissa paste
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the red pepper and garlic in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4 minutes.

  2. 2

    Stir in the harissa, then add the chickpeas and chopped tomatoes.

  3. 3

    Simmer for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve with the crusty bread for scooping.

New Zealand-Style Pan-Fried Snapper with Kumara Wedges

A single portion of New Zealand cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsnapper or sea bream fillet
Fresh produce
  • 200gkumara (sweet potato), cut into wedges
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • ½lemon
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 15gbutter
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchsalt and pepper
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200C (fan 180C). Toss the kumara wedges in 1 tablespoon of the oil and a pinch of salt, and roast for 20 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and golden.

  2. 2

    Pat the fish dry and season both sides.

  3. 3

    Heat the remaining oil and the butter in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the fish for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through, adding the garlic for the last minute.

  4. 4

    Squeeze the lemon over the fish and scatter with parsley. Serve with the kumara wedges.

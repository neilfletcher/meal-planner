Puerto Rican-Style Codfish Fritters (Bacalaitos-Inspired)

A single portion of Puerto Rican cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gcod fillet, flaked
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
  • ½lime
Store cupboard
  • 80gplain flour
  • ½ tspbaking powder
  • 100mlwater
  • 4 tbspvegetable oil, for frying
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Poach or steam the cod for 4–5 minutes until just cooked, then flake into small pieces.

  2. 2

    Whisk the flour, baking powder, cumin and water together to make a thick batter, and season well.

  3. 3

    Stir the flaked cod, garlic and coriander into the batter.

  4. 4

    Heat a shallow layer of vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and spoon in the batter to form 3–4 fritters. Fry for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crisp.

  5. 5

    Drain briefly on kitchen paper and serve with a squeeze of lime.

Ethiopian Beef Tibs with Peppers and Rosemary

A single portion of Ethiopian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • ½green pepper, sliced
  • 2garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1 sprigfresh rosemary
  • 1tomato, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspberbere spice blend
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a hot pan or wok and sear the beef strips for 2–3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

  2. 2

    Add the onion and peppers to the pan and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened and starting to char.

  3. 3

    Add the garlic, ginger, rosemary and berbere, and cook for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Return the beef to the pan with the tomato, toss together and cook for 2–3 minutes until the beef is cooked through.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve with warm flatbread.

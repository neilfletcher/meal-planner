Canadian-Style Maple Salmon with Wild Rice

A single portion of Canadian cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
15 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsalmon fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1lemon wedge
  • 80ggreen beans
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspmaple syrup
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 150gcooked wild rice (pouch)
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the maple syrup, soy sauce and garlic for the glaze.

  2. 2

    Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the salmon, skin-side down, for 4 minutes.

  3. 3

    Flip, brush with the glaze, and cook for 3–4 minutes more until glazed and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, steam the green beans for 4–5 minutes and warm the wild rice.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the salmon over the rice with the green beans, spooning over any extra glaze, with the lemon wedge for squeezing.

Colombian-Style Ajiaco Chicken and Potato Soup with Corn

A single portion of Colombian cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
25 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken breast
Fresh produce
  • 100gwaxy potato, diced
  • 100gfloury potato, diced
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • ¼avocado, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspsoured cream
Frozen
  • 80gsweetcorn kernels
Store cupboard
  • 400mlchicken stock
  • 1 tspcapers
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspdried oregano
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Put the chicken, stock, garlic and half the diced potato into a pan, bring to a simmer and cook for 12–15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  2. 2

    Remove the chicken, shred it, and return to the pan along with the remaining potato and the corn.

  3. 3

    Simmer for a further 10–12 minutes, mashing some of the potato against the side of the pan to thicken the soup slightly.

  4. 4

    Stir in the oregano and spring onion, and season to taste.

  5. 5

    Ladle into a bowl and top with the capers, a spoonful of soured cream, and the avocado slices.

German-Style Pork Schnitzel with Potato Salad

A single portion of German cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gpork loin steak, flattened
Fresh produce
  • 150gnew potatoes, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg, beaten
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspplain flour
  • 3 tbspbreadcrumbs
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes for 12 minutes until tender, then drain and slice while warm.

  2. 2

    Coat the pork in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.

  3. 3

    Fry in a generous splash of oil over medium heat for 3–4 minutes each side until golden and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Toss the warm potatoes with the mustard and a splash of oil, and serve alongside.

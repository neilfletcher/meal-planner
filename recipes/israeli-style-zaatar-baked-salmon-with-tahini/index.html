Israeli-Style Za'atar Baked Salmon with Tahini

A single portion of Israeli cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
14 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsalmon fillet
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 handfulcherry tomatoes
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbsptahini
  • 100gcouscous
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tbspza'atar
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200C (fan 180C).

  2. 2

    Rub the salmon with the oil and za'atar and place on a tray with the cherry tomatoes.

  3. 3

    Bake for 12–14 minutes until the salmon flakes easily and the tomatoes have softened.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile mix the tahini with the garlic, a squeeze of lemon juice and enough water to loosen to a drizzling consistency.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Cook the couscous according to the packet instructions and serve alongside the salmon and tomatoes, drizzled with the tahini sauce.

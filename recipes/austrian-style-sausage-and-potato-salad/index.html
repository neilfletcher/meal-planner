Austrian-Style Sausage & Potato Salad

A single portion of Austrian cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
12 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2frankfurter or pork sausages
Fresh produce
  • 150gnew potatoes, sliced
  • ½onion, thinly sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes for 12 minutes until tender, then drain and slice while warm.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, warm the sausages in simmering water or fry for 6–7 minutes until hot through.

  3. 3

    Whisk the mustard and vinegar with a splash of oil, and toss through the warm potatoes and onion.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Slice the sausages and serve over the potato salad.

New Orleans-Style Blackened Fish with Dirty Rice

A single portion of American cooking, ready in 25 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
16 min
Total
25 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1white fish fillet, about 180g (e.g. haddock)
  • 30gchorizo or smoked sausage, finely diced
Fresh produce
  • ½green pepper, diced
  • ¼onion, diced
  • 1 stickcelery, diced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 150gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tsppaprika
  • ½ tspcayenne pepper
  • ½ tspgarlic powder
  • ½ tspdried thyme
  • ½ tspdried oregano
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the paprika, cayenne, garlic powder, thyme and oregano together and press firmly onto both sides of the fish fillet.

  2. 2

    Heat half the oil in a frying pan over high heat and cook the fish for 2–3 minutes per side until blackened and cooked through. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Add the remaining oil to the pan and fry the chorizo, onion, pepper and celery for 4–5 minutes until softened.

  4. 4

    Stir in the cooked rice and toss well for 2–3 minutes to heat through and pick up the spiced oil.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then serve the dirty rice topped with the blackened fish.

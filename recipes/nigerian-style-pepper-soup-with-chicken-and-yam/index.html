Nigerian-Style Pepper Soup with Chicken and Yam

A single portion of Nigerian cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
25 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken thighs, skin removed
Fresh produce
  • 150gsweet potato (or yam), peeled and cubed
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • ½scotch bonnet or bird's eye chilli, finely chopped
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 400mlchicken stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground nutmeg
  • 2whole cloves
  • ½ tspcayenne pepper
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Put the chicken, onion, garlic, ginger, chilli, nutmeg, cloves and cayenne into a saucepan with the stock.

  2. 2

    Bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the sweet potato and simmer for a further 10–12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sweet potato is tender.

  4. 4

    Season to taste with salt.

  5. 5

    Ladle into a bowl and scatter with the spring onion and coriander to serve.

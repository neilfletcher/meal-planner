Filipino-Style Sinigang na Baboy (Sour Pork & Vegetable Soup)

A single portion of Filipino cooking, ready in 27 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
18 min
Total
27 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gpork tenderloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
  • ½tomato, chopped
  • ½radish (mooli), sliced
  • 50ggreen beans, halved
  • 1 handfulspinach
  • 1green chilli, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 400mlwater or light stock
  • 1 tbsptamarind paste
  • 1 tbspfish sauce
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Simmer the onion and tomato in the water or stock for 8 minutes.

  2. 2

    Stir in the tamarind paste until dissolved.

  3. 3

    Add the radish and green beans, and simmer for a further 6–8 minutes until tender.

  4. 4

    Add the pork and simmer for 3–4 minutes until cooked through. Stir in the spinach and chilli, and season with fish sauce.

  5. 5

    Serve hot in a deep bowl.

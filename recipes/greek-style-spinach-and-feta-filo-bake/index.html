Greek-Style Spinach & Feta Filo Bake

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 100gspinach
Dairy and eggs
  • 60gfeta cheese, crumbled
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Bakery
  • 2sheets filo pastry
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Wilt the spinach in a dry pan for 2 minutes, then squeeze out excess water and chop.

  2. 2

    Mix the spinach with the feta and egg.

  3. 3

    Layer the filo sheets, brushing with oil between each, spoon the filling in and fold into a parcel.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Bake at 200°C (fan 180°C) for 12–14 minutes until golden and crisp.

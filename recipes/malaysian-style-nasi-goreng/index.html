Malaysian-Style Nasi Goreng

A single portion of Malaysian cooking, ready in 15 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
7 min
Cook
8 min
Total
15 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gchicken breast, diced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Frozen
  • 60gfrozen peas
Store cupboard
  • 150gcooked rice, cold
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the chicken in a splash of oil over high heat for 5 minutes until cooked through, then push to one side.

  2. 2

    Add the cold rice, peas, soy sauce and chilli flakes, and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until hot through.

  3. 3

    Push everything to one side, crack in the egg and scramble until just set, then mix through.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve hot.

Jamaican-Style Jerk Pork Chops

A single portion of Jamaican cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2pork chops
Fresh produce
  • ½lime
Frozen
  • 60gfrozen peas
Store cupboard
  • 70grice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspjerk seasoning
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice with the peas according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Rub the pork chops with the jerk seasoning and a squeeze of lime.

  3. 3

    Fry or griddle over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes each side until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Serve with the rice and peas.

Sesame Ginger Beef Stir-Fry

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
8 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 130gbeef frying strips
Fresh produce
  • 150gmixed stir-fry vegetables
Store cupboard
  • 3 tbspshop-bought sesame ginger stir-fry sauce
  • 70gnoodles
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions and drain.

  2. 2

    Heat the vegetable oil in a wok over high heat until smoking, then add the beef and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Add the vegetables to the wok and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until just tender.

  4. 4

    Return the beef to the wok with the stir-fry sauce, and toss everything together for 1 minute.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Add the noodles, toss well to combine, and serve immediately.

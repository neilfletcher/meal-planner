Mexican-Style Chicken Tinga Tacos

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken breast, shredded after cooking
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tspchipotle paste
Bakery
  • 2soft tortillas
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Poach or fry the chicken until cooked through, about 8 minutes, then shred with two forks.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4 minutes.

  3. 3

    Stir in the chipotle paste and chopped tomatoes, add the shredded chicken and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve in the tortillas, scattered with coriander.

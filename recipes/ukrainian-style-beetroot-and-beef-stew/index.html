Ukrainian-Style Beetroot & Beef Stew

A single portion of Ukrainian cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
14 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef rump steak, cut into strips
Fresh produce
  • 100gcooked beetroot, diced
  • ½onion, diced
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 200mlbeef stock
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Sear the beef in a splash of oil over high heat for 1–2 minutes until browned but still pink inside. Tip onto a plate.

  2. 2

    Add the onion and cook over a medium heat for 4–5 minutes, then pour in the stock and simmer for 5 minutes to reduce a little.

  3. 3

    Stir in the beetroot and warm through for 2 minutes, then return the beef for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve with a dollop of soured cream.

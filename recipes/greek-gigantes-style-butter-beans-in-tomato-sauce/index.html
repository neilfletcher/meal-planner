Greek Gigantes-Style Butter Beans in Tomato Sauce

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 27 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
7 min
Cook
20 min
Total
27 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 handfulfresh parsley, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 400gbutter beans (tinned), drained
  • 200gchopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried oregano
  • ½ tspsweet paprika
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread, to serve
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the olive oil in a saucepan and sauté the onion and garlic for 3–4 minutes until softened.

  2. 2

    Stir in the chopped tomatoes, oregano and paprika and bring to a simmer.

  3. 3

    Add the drained butter beans and simmer gently for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened and the beans are heated through.

  4. 4

    Season to taste and scatter with chopped parsley.

  5. 5

    Serve with crusty bread.

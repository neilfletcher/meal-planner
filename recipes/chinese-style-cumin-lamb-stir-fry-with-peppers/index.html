Chinese-Style Cumin Lamb Stir-Fry with Peppers

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150glamb leg steak, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1red pepper, sliced
  • ½white onion, sliced
  • 2garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbsplight soy sauce
  • 1 tspShaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
  • 2 tbspvegetable oil
  • 150gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspcumin seeds
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the lamb with the soy sauce and Shaoxing wine and set aside to marinate briefly.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a wok over high heat, add the lamb and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Add the onion and pepper to the wok and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until starting to soften.

  4. 4

    Add the garlic, cumin seeds and chilli flakes and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.

  5. 5

    Return the lamb to the wok and toss everything together for 1–2 minutes until piping hot.

  6. 6

    Season to taste, then scatter with coriander and serve with rice.

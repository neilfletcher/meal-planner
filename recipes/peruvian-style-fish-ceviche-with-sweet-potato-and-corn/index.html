Peruvian-Style Fish Ceviche with Sweet Potato and Corn

A single portion of Peruvian cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gfirm white fish fillet (sea bass, bream or cod), diced
Fresh produce
  • 4limes, juiced
  • ¼red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1red chilli, finely sliced
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
  • 1small sweet potato, peeled and cubed
  • ½corn on the cob
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 pinchsalt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Use fish sold for eating raw, or fish that has been frozen first (check the label). The lime juice firms the fish but does not cook it.

  2. 2

    Boil the sweet potato and corn in salted water for 10–12 minutes until tender, then drain and cool slightly.

  3. 3

    Toss the diced fish with the lime juice, red onion, chilli and salt.

  4. 4

    Leave to marinate in the fridge for 8–10 minutes, until the fish turns opaque and firms up.

  5. 5

    Stir through the coriander and serve with the sweet potato and corn alongside.

Spiced Chickpea & Spinach Curry

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, finely chopped
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • large handfulbaby spinach
Store cupboard
  • 200gchickpeas, drained
  • 200gchopped tomatoes
  • 60gbasmati rice
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspgaram masala
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Finely chop the onion, garlic and ginger.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat and fry the onion for 3–4 minutes until soft.

  4. 4

    Add the garlic, ginger, garam masala and cumin, and fry for 1 minute until fragrant.

  5. 5

    Stir in the chopped tomatoes and chickpeas, and simmer for 6–8 minutes until thickened.

  6. 6

    Stir through the spinach until wilted, season to taste, and serve with the rice.

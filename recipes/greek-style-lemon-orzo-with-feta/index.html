Greek-Style Lemon Orzo with Feta

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
12 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 80gcherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½lemon
  • small handfulfresh dill or parsley
Dairy and eggs
  • 50gfeta, crumbled
Store cupboard
  • 70gorzo
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the orzo in salted boiling water according to the packet instructions, then drain, reserving a splash of the cooking water.

  2. 2

    Toss the warm orzo with the olive oil, lemon zest and a squeeze of juice, loosening with the reserved water if needed.

  3. 3

    Stir through the cherry tomatoes and most of the feta.

  4. 4

    Scatter with the remaining feta and dill or parsley to serve.

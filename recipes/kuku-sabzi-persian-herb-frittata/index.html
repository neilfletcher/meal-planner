Kuku Sabzi (Persian Herb Frittata)

A single portion of Persian cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1 handfulfresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 small handfulfresh dill, chopped
  • 2spring onions, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 3eggs
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspplain flour
  • 1 tbspchopped walnuts
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspturmeric
  • pinchsalt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Whisk the eggs with the turmeric, flour and a pinch of salt until smooth.

  2. 2

    Stir in all the chopped herbs, spring onions and walnuts.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a small non-stick pan over medium heat and pour in the mixture.

  4. 4

    Cook for 4–5 minutes until the base is set and golden, then flip using a plate, or finish under the grill for 3–4 minutes until fully set.

  5. 5

    Slide onto a plate, cut into wedges and serve warm or at room temperature.

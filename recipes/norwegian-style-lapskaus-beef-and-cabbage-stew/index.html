Norwegian-Style Lapskaus (Beef and Cabbage Stew)

A single portion of Norwegian cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
25 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef rump steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1carrot, sliced
  • 1potato, cut into 1.5cm cubes
  • 80gwhite cabbage, shredded
  • 1 tbspfresh parsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspbutter
Store cupboard
  • 250mlbeef stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1bay leaf
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a pot over high heat and sear the beef for 1–2 minutes until browned. Tip onto a plate.

  2. 2

    Soften the onion and carrot in the same pot for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the potato, stock and bay leaf, cover and simmer for 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the cabbage and simmer for 6–8 minutes until the potato and cabbage are tender.

  5. 5

    Return the beef and its juices for 1–2 minutes, season and scatter with parsley to serve.

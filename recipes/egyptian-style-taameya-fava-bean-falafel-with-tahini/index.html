Egyptian-Style Ta'ameya Fava Bean Falafel with Tahini

A single portion of Egyptian cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
8 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½small onion, roughly chopped
  • 2garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 15gfresh coriander, chopped
  • 15gfresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsplemon juice
Store cupboard
  • 200gtinned broad beans, drained and skins removed
  • ½ tspbaking powder
  • 2 tbspplain flour
  • 3 tbspvegetable oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsptahini
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • 1 tspground coriander
Bakery
  • 1pitta bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Blitz the broad beans, onion, garlic, herbs, cumin and ground coriander in a food processor to a coarse paste.

  2. 2

    Stir in the flour and baking powder, season well, then shape into 5–6 small patties.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a frying pan and shallow-fry the patties for 2–3 minutes each side until golden and crisp.

  4. 4

    Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice and a splash of water to a drizzling consistency.

  5. 5

    Serve the patties in warmed pitta with the tahini sauce spooned over.

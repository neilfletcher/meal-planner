Singapore-Style Bak Kut Teh (Peppery Pork Rib Soup)

A single portion of Singaporean cooking, ready in 83 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
75 min
Total
83 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 200gpork ribs
Fresh produce
  • 3garlic cloves, whole
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspdark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsplight soy sauce
  • 400mlstock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspwhite peppercorns, lightly crushed
  • 1star anise
  • ½cinnamon stick
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toast the peppercorns, star anise and cinnamon in a dry pot over medium heat for 1 minute until fragrant.

  2. 2

    Add the stock, garlic, pork ribs and both soy sauces.

  3. 3

    Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently for 60–75 minutes until the pork is very tender, skimming occasionally and topping up with water if needed.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning, then scatter with spring onion and coriander to serve.

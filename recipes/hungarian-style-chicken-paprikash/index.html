Hungarian-Style Chicken Paprikash

A single portion of Hungarian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 70gegg noodles or pasta
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tbspsweet paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4 minutes until soft.

  3. 3

    Add the chicken and cook for 6–7 minutes until browned.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Take off the heat and stir in the paprika (it burns easily), then add the chopped tomatoes and simmer for 4 minutes, then stir through the soured cream and serve over the noodles.

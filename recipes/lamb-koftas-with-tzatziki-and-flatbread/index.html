Lamb Koftas with Tzatziki and Flatbread

A single portion of Greek cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 4 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
4 min
Cook
10 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 4shop-bought lamb koftas
Fresh produce
  • small handfulfresh mint, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspolive oil
  • 4 tbspshop-bought tzatziki
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the koftas for 8–10 minutes, turning regularly, until browned all over and cooked through.

  2. 2

    Warm the flatbread.

  3. 3

    Season to taste, then serve the koftas with the flatbread and tzatziki, scattered with a few mint leaves.

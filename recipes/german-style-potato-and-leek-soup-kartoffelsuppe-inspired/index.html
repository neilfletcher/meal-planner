German-Style Potato & Leek Soup (Kartoffelsuppe-Inspired)

A single portion of German cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 200gpotato, diced
  • 1leek, sliced
  • 1 tbspchives, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 15gbutter
  • 2 tbspmilk or single cream
Store cupboard
  • 400mlvegetable stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 pinchnutmeg, grated
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a pan and soften the leek for 4–5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add the potato and stock, bring to a simmer, and cook for 15–18 minutes until the potato is tender.

  3. 3

    Roughly mash some of the potato in the pan to thicken the soup slightly.

  4. 4

    Stir in the milk or cream and nutmeg, and season to taste.

  5. 5

    Top with chives to serve.

Uzbek-Style Manti Dumplings with Soured Cream

A single portion of Uzbek cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 10 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
10 min
Cook
14 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100glamb mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, very finely chopped
  • 1 tbspdill, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspbutter, melted
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 10fresh dumpling or wonton wrappers
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • pinchblack pepper
  • pinchpaprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the lamb mince with the onion, cumin and pepper.

  2. 2

    Place a teaspoon of filling in the centre of each wrapper, dampen the edges with water and pinch closed into small parcels.

  3. 3

    Steam the dumplings in a steamer basket over simmering water for 12–14 minutes until the lamb is cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Drizzle with the melted butter and serve topped with soured cream, a scattering of dill and a pinch of paprika.

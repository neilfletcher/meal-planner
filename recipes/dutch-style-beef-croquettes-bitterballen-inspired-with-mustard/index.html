Dutch-Style Beef Croquettes (Bitterballen-Inspired) with Mustard

A single portion of Dutch cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gcooked roast beef or beef mince, finely chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 15gbutter
  • 1egg, beaten
Store cupboard
  • 15gplain flour
  • 150mlbeef stock
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 40gbreadcrumbs
  • 3 tbspvegetable oil, for frying
  • 1 tspmustard, to serve
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchnutmeg, grated
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the butter in a small pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute to make a roux.

  2. 2

    Gradually whisk in the stock until smooth and thick, then stir in the chopped beef, mustard and nutmeg. Cook for 2–3 minutes until very thick.

  3. 3

    Spread the mixture onto a plate and chill in the freezer for 15 minutes until firm enough to shape.

  4. 4

    Roll into 4–5 balls, dip each in beaten egg then breadcrumbs.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the croquettes for 3–4 minutes, turning, until deep golden and crisp. Serve hot with mustard.

Teriyaki Salmon with Sesame Greens

A single portion of Japanese cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 4 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
4 min
Cook
10 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsalmon fillet
Fresh produce
  • 100gtenderstem broccoli or greens
Store cupboard
  • 3 tbspshop-bought teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tspsesame seeds
  • 70gjasmine rice
  • 1 tspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat and fry the salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes, then flip and cook for 2 minutes more.

  3. 3

    Pour the teriyaki sauce into the pan and let it bubble for 1–2 minutes, spooning it over the salmon until glossy.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, steam or blanch the greens for 3–4 minutes until just tender.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the salmon and its glaze over the rice with the greens, scattered with sesame seeds.

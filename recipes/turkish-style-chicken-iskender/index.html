Turkish-Style Chicken Iskender

A single portion of Turkish cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspnatural yoghurt
  • 1 tbspbutter
Store cupboard
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the chicken in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 7–8 minutes until browned and cooked through.

  2. 2

    Warm the chopped tomatoes in a separate small pan for 4 minutes.

  3. 3

    Tear the flatbread onto a plate, top with the chicken and tomato sauce.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Melt the butter and drizzle over, with a dollop of yoghurt on the side.

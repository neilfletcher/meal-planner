German-Style Chicken Jäger-Style with Creamy Mushroom Sauce

A single portion of German cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken breast, flattened
Fresh produce
  • 100gchestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg, beaten
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
  • 15gbutter
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspplain flour
  • 30gbreadcrumbs
  • 100mlchicken stock
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Coat the chicken in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the chicken for 4–5 minutes each side until golden and cooked through. Remove and keep warm.

  3. 3

    In the same pan, melt the butter and fry the onion and mushrooms for 4–5 minutes until softened.

  4. 4

    Add the stock and simmer for 2–3 minutes to reduce slightly, then stir in the soured cream.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then spoon the mushroom sauce over the schnitzel and scatter with parsley.

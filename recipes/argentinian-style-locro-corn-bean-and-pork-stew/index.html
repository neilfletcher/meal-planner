Argentinian-Style Locro Corn, Bean and Pork Stew

A single portion of Argentinian cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
25 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gpork loin steak, diced small
  • 40gchorizo, sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 100gbutternut squash, diced
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 100gtinned sweetcorn
  • 100gtinned butter beans, drained
  • 200mlchicken stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • pinchchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat a splash of oil in a pan and brown the pork and chorizo for 2–3 minutes, then tip onto a plate.

  2. 2

    Add the onion and garlic and soften for 2 minutes, then stir in the paprika and cumin and cook for 30 seconds.

  3. 3

    Add the squash, sweetcorn, beans and stock. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until the squash is tender and the stew has thickened.

  4. 4

    Return the pork and chorizo for the last 4–5 minutes until the pork is cooked through. Season to taste and scatter with spring onion and chilli flakes to serve.

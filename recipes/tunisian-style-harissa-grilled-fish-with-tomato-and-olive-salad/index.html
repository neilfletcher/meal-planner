Tunisian-Style Harissa-Grilled Fish with Tomato & Olive Salad

A single portion of Tunisian cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gwhite fish fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1lemon, juiced
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • ¼cucumber, chopped
  • ¼red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspharissa paste
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbspblack olives
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the harissa, half the oil and half the lemon juice, and coat the fish.

  2. 2

    Grill or fry the fish for 3–4 minutes each side until cooked through.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, toss the tomato, cucumber, onion, olives and parsley with the remaining oil, lemon juice and cumin.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the fish on top of the salad.

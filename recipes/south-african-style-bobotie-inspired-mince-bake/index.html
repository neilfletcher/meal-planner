South African-Style Bobotie-Inspired Mince Bake

A single portion of South African cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 2 tbspmilk
Store cupboard
  • 20graisins
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspmild curry powder
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the onion and beef mince in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes, breaking up the mince.

  2. 2

    Stir in the curry powder and raisins, then spoon into a small ovenproof dish.

  3. 3

    Whisk the egg with the milk and pour over the top.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Bake at 190°C (fan 170°C) for 12–14 minutes until the egg topping is set and golden.

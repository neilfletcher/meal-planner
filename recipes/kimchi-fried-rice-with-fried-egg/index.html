Kimchi Fried Rice with Fried Egg

A single portion of Korean cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
10 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 150gcooked rice, cold
  • 80gkimchi, chopped
  • 1 tbspkimchi juice (from the jar)
  • 1 tspsesame oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat a splash of oil in a frying pan or wok over high heat and fry the kimchi for 2 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add the cold rice and kimchi juice, and stir-fry for 4–5 minutes, breaking up any clumps, until hot through and starting to crisp.

  3. 3

    Push to one side of the pan, fry the egg in the space, keeping the yolk runny.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then drizzle the rice with sesame oil, top with the egg, and scatter with sliced spring onion.

Russian-Style Chicken & Mushroom Skillet

A single portion of Russian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • 100gchestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 70grice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the chicken, mushrooms and onion in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 8–9 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  3. 3

    Stir in the soured cream and mustard, and warm through for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the rice.

Pakistani-Style Chicken Karahi

A single portion of Pakistani cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • 1green chilli, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground ginger
  • ½ tspground cumin
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the chicken in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 5 minutes until browned.

  2. 2

    Stir in the ginger and cumin, then add the chopped tomatoes and green chilli.

  3. 3

    Simmer for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve with the flatbread.

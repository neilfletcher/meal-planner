Gujarati Chickpea Flour Kadhi with Rice

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
7 min
Cook
15 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1green chilli, chopped
  • 6fresh curry leaves
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 150gnatural yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspgram (chickpea) flour
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 1 tspsugar
  • 120gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspturmeric
  • ½ tspmustard seeds
  • ¼ tspcumin seeds
  • pinchasafoetida
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Whisk the yoghurt with the gram flour, turmeric and 200ml water until completely smooth.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and splutter the mustard and cumin seeds, then add the curry leaves, asafoetida, ginger and chilli.

  3. 3

    Pour in the yoghurt mixture and bring to a gentle simmer, stirring constantly so it doesn't split. Stir in the sugar.

  4. 4

    Simmer for 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until slightly thickened.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  7. 7

    Serve the kadhi over the rice, scattered with coriander.

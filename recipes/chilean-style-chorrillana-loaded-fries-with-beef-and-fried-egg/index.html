Chilean-Style Chorrillana Loaded Fries with Beef and Fried Egg

A single portion of Chilean cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
18 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gbeef sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 40gcheddar, grated
Frozen
  • 200gfrozen chips
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the chips in the oven according to the packet instructions until golden and crisp.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, heat a little oil in a frying pan and fry the onion until soft and starting to caramelise. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Season the beef with the paprika, add to the hot pan and fry for 2–3 minutes until browned.

  4. 4

    Fry the egg in a separate pan to your liking.

  5. 5

    Pile the chips onto a plate, top with the beef, onion and grated cheese, then finish with the fried egg.

Smoked Mackerel & Beetroot Salad

A single portion of British cooking, ready in 6 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
0 min
Total
6 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gsmoked mackerel fillet
Fresh produce
  • 100gcooked beetroot
  • large handfulbaby spinach or rocket
  • ¼lemon
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspnatural yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 1 tsphorseradish sauce
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Dice the beetroot and flake the mackerel into large pieces, discarding the skin.

  2. 2

    Whisk the horseradish and yoghurt together with a squeeze of lemon to make a dressing.

  3. 3

    Arrange the spinach or rocket on a plate, top with the beetroot and flaked mackerel.

  4. 4

    Drizzle with the horseradish dressing and serve with crusty bread.

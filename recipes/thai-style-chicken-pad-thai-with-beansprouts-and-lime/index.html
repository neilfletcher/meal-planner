Thai-Style Chicken Pad Thai with Beansprouts and Lime

A single portion of Thai cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 130gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • 60gbeansprouts
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1lime wedge
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 100gflat rice noodles
  • 1 tbsptamarind paste
  • 1 tbspfish sauce
  • 1 tspsoft brown sugar
  • 2 tbspvegetable oil
  • 1 tbspcrushed roasted peanuts
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchdried chilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Soak the rice noodles in boiled water according to packet instructions until just tender, then drain.

  2. 2

    Mix the tamarind paste, fish sauce and sugar in a small bowl to make the sauce.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 3–4 minutes until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Push to one side, crack in the egg and scramble briefly, then add the garlic and noodles and toss together.

  5. 5

    Pour in the sauce, add the beansprouts and spring onion, and toss for 1–2 minutes until well coated and hot.

  6. 6

    Season to taste, then serve scattered with the crushed peanuts, chilli flakes, and the lime wedge.

Chinese-Style Steamed Fish with Ginger

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gwhite fish fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1spring onion, shredded
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • ½ tspsesame oil
  • 70grice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Place the fish on a plate with the ginger, and steam over a pan of simmering water, covered, for 8–9 minutes until just cooked through.

  3. 3

    Scatter with spring onion, drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the rice.

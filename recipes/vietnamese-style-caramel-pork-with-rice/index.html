Vietnamese-Style Caramel Pork with Rice

A single portion of Vietnamese cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gpork mince
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 ½ tbspfish sauce
  • 1 ½ tbspbrown sugar
  • 150gcooked rice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the sugar in a dry saucepan over medium heat until it turns a deep caramel colour, watching closely so it doesn't burn.

  2. 2

    Add the pork mince and brown for 3–4 minutes, breaking it up as it cooks.

  3. 3

    Stir in the fish sauce, garlic and ginger with a splash of water, and simmer for 5 minutes until sticky.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the rice, scattered with sliced spring onion.

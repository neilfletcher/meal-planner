Lemon Herb Chicken Thigh Traybake with New Potatoes

A single portion of British cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
28 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gboneless chicken thighs
Fresh produce
  • 150gnew potatoes, halved
  • ½lemon
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried mixed herbs
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Toss the potatoes with half the oil on a baking tray and roast for 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    Toss the chicken with the remaining oil, the dried herbs, crushed garlic and lemon zest, then add to the tray with the potatoes.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Squeeze over the lemon juice and roast for a further 16–18 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender.

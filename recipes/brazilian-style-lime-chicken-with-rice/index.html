Brazilian-Style Lime Chicken with Rice

A single portion of Brazilian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • 1lime
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 70grice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Toss the chicken with the paprika, garlic and a squeeze of lime juice.

  3. 3

    Fry in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 7–8 minutes until browned and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve over the rice with the remaining lime and coriander.

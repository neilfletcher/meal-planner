Hawaiian-Style Salmon Poke Bowl

A single portion of Hawaiian cooking, ready in 9 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
0 min
Total
9 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gsushi-grade salmon, cubed
Fresh produce
  • ½avocado, sliced
  • 60gcucumber, diced
Store cupboard
  • 150gcooked rice, cold
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • ½ tspsesame oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the salmon cubes with the soy sauce and sesame oil.

  2. 2

    Spoon the cold rice into a bowl.

  3. 3

    Top with the marinated salmon, avocado and cucumber.

  4. 4

    Serve straight away.

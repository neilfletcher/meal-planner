Swiss Cheese Fondue for One

A single portion of Swiss cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, halved
  • 1 tsplemon juice
  • 1small potato, boiled
Dairy and eggs
  • 80gGruyère, grated
  • 40gEmmental, grated
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspcornflour
  • 60mlwhite wine or apple juice
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchground nutmeg
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread, cubed
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potato in salted water for 12–15 minutes until tender, then drain and cut into chunks. Meanwhile, rub the inside of a small pan with the cut garlic clove.

  2. 2

    Warm the wine or apple juice gently over low heat.

  3. 3

    Toss the grated cheeses with the cornflour, then add gradually to the pan, stirring constantly until melted and smooth.

  4. 4

    Stir in the lemon juice and a pinch of nutmeg.

  5. 5

    Serve immediately with the cubed bread and boiled potato for dipping.

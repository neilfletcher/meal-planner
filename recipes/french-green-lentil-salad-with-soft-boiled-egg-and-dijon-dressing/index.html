French Green Lentil Salad with Soft-Boiled Egg and Dijon Dressing

A single portion of French cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
8 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 20grocket
  • 60gcherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1small shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 250gready-cooked puy lentils
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 2 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Bring a small pan of water to the boil and cook the egg for 6–7 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.

  2. 2

    Warm the lentils in a pan or microwave according to the pack instructions.

  3. 3

    Whisk the Dijon mustard, vinegar, olive oil and shallot together for the dressing.

  4. 4

    Toss the warm lentils with the rocket, tomatoes and dressing.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Halve the egg and place on top, scattering with parsley to serve.

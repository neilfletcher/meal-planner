Korean-Style Braised Mackerel with Gochugaru (Godeungeo Jorim)

A single portion of Korean cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1mackerel fillet, about 150g
Fresh produce
  • ½daikon radish or 1 potato, thinly sliced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tspgochujang
  • 1 tspsugar
  • 100mlwater
  • 150gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tbspgochugaru
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Layer the sliced daikon (or potato) in the base of a small pan.

  2. 2

    Mix the soy sauce, gochugaru, gochujang, garlic, ginger, sugar and water together and pour half over the vegetables.

  3. 3

    Lay the mackerel fillet on top, skin-side up, and pour over the remaining sauce.

  4. 4

    Bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 10–12 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the fish is cooked through, spooning sauce over occasionally.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Scatter with spring onion and serve with rice.

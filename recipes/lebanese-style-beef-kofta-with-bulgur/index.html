Lebanese-Style Beef Kofta with Bulgur

A single portion of Lebanese cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
9 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • small handfulfresh parsley, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 60gbulgur wheat
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the bulgur wheat according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Mix the mince with the cumin and cinnamon, and shape into small koftas.

  3. 3

    Fry over medium-high heat for 7–8 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve over the bulgur, scattered with parsley.

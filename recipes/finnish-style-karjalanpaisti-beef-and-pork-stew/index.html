Finnish-Style Karjalanpaisti (Beef and Pork Stew)

A single portion of Finnish cooking, ready in 31 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
22 min
Total
31 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 90gbeef rump steak, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 90gpork loin steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • 1onion, sliced
  • 1carrot, sliced
  • 150gpotatoes, halved
Dairy and eggs
  • 15gbutter
Store cupboard
  • 250mlbeef stock
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspwhole black peppercorns
  • 1bay leaf
  • to tastesalt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15–18 minutes until tender.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, heat the butter in a saucepan over high heat and sear the beef and pork for 2–3 minutes until browned. Tip onto a plate.

  3. 3

    Add the onion and carrot and cook for 3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the peppercorns, bay leaf and stock and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes until the carrot is tender and the liquid has reduced a little.

  5. 5

    Return the meat and its juices and simmer for 2–3 minutes until the pork is cooked through. Season with salt, remove the bay leaf and serve with the potatoes.

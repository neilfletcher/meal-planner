Polish Dill Chicken with Soured Cream

A single portion of Polish cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • handfulfresh dill, chopped
  • 70gnew potatoes
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspplain flour
  • 100mlchicken stock
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the new potatoes for 12 minutes until tender, then drain.

  2. 2

    Fry the chicken in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes until browned and cooked through.

  3. 3

    Stir in the flour, then pour in the stock and simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Stir in the soured cream and most of the dill, and serve over the potatoes, scattered with the rest of the dill.

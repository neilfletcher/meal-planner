Dublin Coddle-Style Sausage & Potato Stew

A single portion of Irish cooking, ready in 38 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
30 min
Total
38 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2pork sausages, thickly sliced
  • 2smoked bacon rashers, chopped
Fresh produce
  • 200gpotatoes, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1 tspthyme, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 200mlchicken stock
Spices and seasoning
  • pinchblack pepper
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat a splash of oil in a pan and brown the sausage pieces and bacon for 3–4 minutes.

  2. 2

    Layer the potatoes and onion over the meat, pour in the stock, and add the thyme and a good grind of pepper.

  3. 3

    Cover and simmer gently for 25–30 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the stock has reduced to a thick gravy.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve hot straight from the pot with crusty bread.

Pakistani-Style Chapli Kebab with Yoghurt Chutney

A single portion of Pakistani cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ¼onion, finely diced
  • 1tomato, finely diced (seeds removed)
  • 1green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander, chopped
  • ½ tspfresh mint, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 3 tbspnatural yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspgram flour
  • 2 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspcoriander seeds, crushed
  • 1 tspcumin seeds, crushed
  • ½ tspchilli powder
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the beef mince with the onion, tomato, chilli, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, chilli powder, gram flour and fresh coriander until well combined.

  2. 2

    Shape into 2 flat, wide patties.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat and fry the patties for 4–5 minutes per side until well browned and cooked through.

  4. 4

    Mix the yoghurt with the mint to make a quick chutney.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then serve the chapli kebabs with the minted yoghurt chutney.

Pork with Gremolata (Milanese Ossobuco-Style)

A single portion of Italian cooking, ready in 31 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
22 min
Total
31 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork loin steak
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • ½carrot, diced
  • 1celery stick, diced
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1lemon, zested
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspplain flour
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 100gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 100mlchicken stock
  • 50mlwhite wine
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Dust the pork in flour and season. Heat the oil in a pan over medium-high heat and brown the pork for 2 minutes each side, then remove.

  2. 2

    Add the onion, carrot and celery to the pan and soften for 4–5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the tomatoes, stock and wine and simmer uncovered for 8 minutes. Return the pork, cover and cook gently for 6–8 minutes until just cooked through.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, mix the garlic, lemon zest and parsley together for the gremolata.

  5. 5

    Scatter the gremolata over the pork just before serving.

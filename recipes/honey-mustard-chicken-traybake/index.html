Honey Mustard Chicken Traybake

A single portion of British cooking, ready in 31 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
25 min
Total
31 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gboneless chicken thighs
Fresh produce
  • 150gnew potatoes, halved
  • ½red onion, cut into wedges
  • few sprigsfresh thyme
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspshop-bought honey mustard sauce or dressing
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Halve the new potatoes and cut the red onion into wedges. Toss both with half the olive oil on a baking tray and roast for 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, toss the chicken thighs through the honey mustard sauce to coat.

  4. 4

    Push the potatoes to one side and add the chicken to the tray, scattering with thyme sprigs.

  5. 5

    Roast for a further 15–18 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender, turning everything halfway.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving.

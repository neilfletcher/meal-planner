Canadian-Style Montreal Smoked Meat Sandwich with Mustard

A single portion of Canadian cooking, ready in 15 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
8 min
Total
15 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gsmoked beef brisket or pastrami-style deli meat, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspdeli mustard
  • 1gherkin
Bakery
  • 2slices rye bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Warm a frying pan or griddle over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Pile the smoked beef into the pan and warm through for 2–3 minutes, tossing, until heated and slightly crisped at the edges.

  3. 3

    Lightly toast the rye bread.

  4. 4

    Spread one slice with mustard, pile on the hot smoked beef, and top with the second slice.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Cut in half and serve with the gherkin on the side.

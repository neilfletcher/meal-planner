Turkish-Style Red Lentil Soup

A single portion of Turkish cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
15 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • ½lemon
Store cupboard
  • 80gdried red lentils
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the onion in a splash of oil over medium heat for 4 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Rinse the lentils, add to the pan with the cumin, paprika and 400ml water.

  3. 3

    Simmer for 12 minutes until the lentils are soft, then blitz or mash until smooth.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve with a squeeze of lemon and the crusty bread.

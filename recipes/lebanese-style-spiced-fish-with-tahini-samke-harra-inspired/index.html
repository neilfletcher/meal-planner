Lebanese-Style Spiced Fish with Tahini (Samke Harra-Inspired)

A single portion of Lebanese cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gwhite fish fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1lemon, juiced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • ½red onion, sliced
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 2 tbsptahini
  • 1 tbsppine nuts
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the fish for 3–4 minutes each side until just cooked. Remove and keep warm.

  2. 2

    Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice, garlic and a splash of water until smooth and pourable.

  3. 3

    In the same pan, soften the onion and pepper with the chilli and cumin for 4–5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then spoon the tahini sauce over the fish, top with the onion and pepper mixture, and scatter with pine nuts and coriander.

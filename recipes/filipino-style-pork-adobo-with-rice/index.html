Filipino-Style Pork Adobo with Rice

A single portion of Filipino cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
14 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork loin steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspsoy sauce
  • 2 tbspwhite or rice vinegar
  • 150gcooked rice
  • 1 tspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1bay leaf
  • ¼ tspblack peppercorns
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Brown the pork in the oil over high heat for 2–3 minutes, then tip onto a plate.

  2. 2

    Add the soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaf, peppercorns and 3 tbsp water to the pan.

  3. 3

    Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes until the sauce has reduced slightly, then return the pork and simmer for 3–4 minutes until cooked through and glazed.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve over the rice.

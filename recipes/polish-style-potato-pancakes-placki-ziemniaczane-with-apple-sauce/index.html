Polish-Style Potato Pancakes (Placki Ziemniaczane) with Apple Sauce

A single portion of Polish cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 200gpotato, peeled and grated
  • ½onion, grated
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 1 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspplain flour
  • 3 tbspvegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 tbspapple sauce
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 pinchsalt
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Squeeze as much liquid as possible out of the grated potato and onion using a clean tea towel.

  2. 2

    Mix with the egg, flour and a pinch of salt.

  3. 3

    Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry spoonfuls of the mixture, flattened slightly, for 3–4 minutes each side until golden and crisp.

  4. 4

    Drain on kitchen paper and serve hot with apple sauce and soured cream.

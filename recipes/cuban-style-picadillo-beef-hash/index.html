Cuban-Style Picadillo Beef Hash

A single portion of Cuban cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
Store cupboard
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 20graisins
  • 70grice
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the onion and beef mince in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes, breaking up the mince.

  3. 3

    Stir in the cumin, raisins and chopped tomatoes, and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve over the rice.

Goan Pork Vindaloo with Rice

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 28 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Total
28 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork loin steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • 1onion, sliced
  • 2garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspvindaloo curry paste
  • 1 tbspmalt vinegar
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 120gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the pork with the vindaloo paste and vinegar in a bowl and set aside to marinate while you prep everything else.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the onion for 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tomato, cumin and 100ml water and simmer for 6–8 minutes until thick.

  4. 4

    Add the pork with its marinade and simmer for 5–6 minutes, stirring, until cooked through. Add a splash of water if it looks dry.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile, cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  7. 7

    Serve the vindaloo over the rice, scattered with coriander.

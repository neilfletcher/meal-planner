Fesenjan-Style Chicken with Pomegranate and Walnut

A single portion of Persian cooking, ready in 30 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
22 min
Total
30 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken thighs
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsppomegranate seeds
Store cupboard
  • 40gwalnuts, finely ground
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 2 tbsppomegranate molasses
  • 150mlchicken stock
  • pinchsugar
  • 70gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and brown the chicken pieces all over, 4–5 minutes. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Soften the onion in the same pan for 4 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the ground walnuts and toast for 1–2 minutes, then stir in the stock, pomegranate molasses and cinnamon.

  5. 5

    Return the chicken to the pan, cover and simmer for 15 minutes until cooked through and the sauce is thick and glossy, adding a pinch of sugar to balance if needed.

  6. 6

    Season to taste, then serve the chicken and sauce over the rice, scattered with pomegranate seeds.

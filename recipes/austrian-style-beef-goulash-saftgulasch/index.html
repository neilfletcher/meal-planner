Austrian-Style Beef Goulash (Saftgulasch)

A single portion of Austrian cooking, ready in 29 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
20 min
Total
29 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef rump steak, cut into strips
Fresh produce
  • 1onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbsptomato puree
  • 200mlbeef stock
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tbspsweet paprika
  • ½ tspcaraway seeds
  • ½ tspdried marjoram
Bakery
  • 1crusty bread roll, to serve
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil over high heat and sear the beef for 1–2 minutes until browned. Tip onto a plate.

  2. 2

    Lower the heat, add the onion and cook for 6–8 minutes until soft and golden.

  3. 3

    Stir in the paprika, caraway and garlic for 1 minute, then add the tomato puree and stock.

  4. 4

    Simmer uncovered for 8–10 minutes until reduced and glossy.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then return the beef and its juices for 1–2 minutes, stir through the marjoram and serve with crusty bread.

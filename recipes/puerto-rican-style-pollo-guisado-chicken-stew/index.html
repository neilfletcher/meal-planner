Puerto Rican-Style Pollo Guisado Chicken Stew

A single portion of Puerto Rican cooking, ready in 34 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
25 min
Total
34 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • ¼onion, diced
  • ¼green pepper, diced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1small potato, diced
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbsptomato purée
  • 150mlchicken stock
  • 1 handfulgreen olives
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried oregano
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the olive oil in a saucepan and brown the chicken pieces over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add the onion, pepper and garlic and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tomato purée, oregano and cumin, cook for 1 minute, then add the stock and potato.

  4. 4

    Bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 15–18 minutes until the potato and chicken are cooked through.

  5. 5

    Stir in the olives, season to taste, and scatter with coriander to serve.

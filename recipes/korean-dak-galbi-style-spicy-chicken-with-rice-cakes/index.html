Korean Dak Galbi-Style Spicy Chicken with Rice Cakes

A single portion of Korean cooking, ready in 25 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
16 min
Total
25 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken thigh, diced
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • ¼white cabbage, shredded
  • ½carrot, sliced
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Frozen
  • 100gKorean rice cakes (tteok)
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspgochujang
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tsphoney
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspgochugaru (Korean chilli flakes)
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, honey, garlic and ginger in a bowl. Toss the chicken in half the sauce.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a frying pan or wok over medium-high heat, add the chicken and stir-fry for 5–6 minutes until browned and nearly cooked through.

  3. 3

    Add the cabbage and carrot and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until starting to soften.

  4. 4

    Add the rice cakes, remaining sauce and a splash of water, and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the rice cakes are soft and the chicken is cooked through.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Scatter with spring onion to serve.

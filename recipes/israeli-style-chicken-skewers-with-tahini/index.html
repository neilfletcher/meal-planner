Israeli-Style Chicken Skewers with Tahini

A single portion of Israeli cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
9 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken breast, cubed
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbsptahini
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the chicken with the cumin, paprika and a splash of oil.

  2. 2

    Thread onto skewers (or leave loose) and griddle or fry over medium-high heat for 8–9 minutes, turning, until cooked through.

  3. 3

    Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice and a splash of water until smooth and drizzly.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the chicken in the flatbread, drizzled with the tahini sauce.

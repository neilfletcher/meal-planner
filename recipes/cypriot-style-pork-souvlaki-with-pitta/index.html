Cypriot-Style Pork Souvlaki with Pitta

A single portion of Cypriot cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
9 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gpork loin, cubed
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspshop-bought tzatziki
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspdried oregano
Bakery
  • 1pitta bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the pork with the oregano, a squeeze of lemon and a splash of oil.

  2. 2

    Fry or griddle over medium-high heat for 8–9 minutes, turning, until cooked through.

  3. 3

    Warm the pitta.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the pork in the pitta with the tzatziki.

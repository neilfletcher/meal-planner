Huevos Rancheros with Refried Beans

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
7 min
Cook
12 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, finely chopped
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 2eggs
  • 30gcheese, grated
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbspvegetable oil
  • 200gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 120gtinned refried beans
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspchilli powder
Bakery
  • 2small corn tortillas
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat half the oil and soften the onion and garlic for 4 minutes. Add the tomatoes and chilli powder, and simmer for 8 minutes to a thick sauce.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile warm the refried beans in a small pan with a splash of water.

  3. 3

    Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.

  4. 4

    Fry the eggs in the remaining oil to your liking.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Spread the beans over the tortillas, top with the tomato sauce, fried eggs, cheese and coriander.

Sri Lankan-Style Black Pepper Beef Curry

A single portion of Sri Lankan cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
18 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 2garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1 sprigfresh curry leaves
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 150mlcoconut milk
  • 100gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspblack peppercorns, coarsely crushed
  • ½ tspground coriander
  • ¼ tspturmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil over high heat and sear the beef for 1–2 minutes until browned. Tip onto a plate.

  3. 3

    Lower the heat and soften the onion, garlic, ginger and curry leaves for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Stir in the black pepper, coriander and turmeric and cook for 1 minute.

  5. 5

    Pour in the coconut milk and 50ml water and simmer uncovered for 6–8 minutes until thickened.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  7. 7

    Return the beef and its juices for 1–2 minutes, then serve with the rice.

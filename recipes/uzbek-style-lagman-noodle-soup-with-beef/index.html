Uzbek-Style Lagman Noodle Soup with Beef

A single portion of Uzbek cooking, ready in 27 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
18 min
Total
27 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef sirloin, cut into strips
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1carrot, sliced
  • ½pepper, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 100gudon noodles
  • 300mlbeef stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Brown the beef strips in a hot pan, then set aside.

  2. 2

    Soften the onion, carrot, pepper and garlic in the same pan for 4–5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the tomato, stock, cumin and chilli flakes, and simmer for 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Return the beef for 1–2 minutes to heat through.

  5. 5

    Meanwhile, cook the noodles according to the packet instructions, drain and divide into a bowl.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  7. 7

    Ladle the soup over the noodles and scatter with coriander.

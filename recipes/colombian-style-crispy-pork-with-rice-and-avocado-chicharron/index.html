Colombian-Style Crispy Pork with Rice and Avocado (Chicharrón)

A single portion of Colombian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork belly slices
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • ½avocado, sliced
  • 1lime, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
  • ¼red onion, thinly sliced
Store cupboard
  • 150gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 pinchsalt
  • ½ tspground cumin
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Season the pork belly with salt, cumin and garlic.

  2. 2

    Fry skin-side down in a hot, dry pan for 8–10 minutes until deeply golden and crisp.

  3. 3

    Turn and cook for a further 4–5 minutes until cooked through. Rest for 2 minutes, then slice.

  4. 4

    Serve over the rice with sliced avocado, red onion, coriander and lime wedges.

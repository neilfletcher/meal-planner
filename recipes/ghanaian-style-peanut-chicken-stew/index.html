Ghanaian-Style Peanut Chicken Stew

A single portion of Ghanaian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 160gchicken thigh, diced
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbsppeanut butter
  • 150gchopped tomatoes
  • 70grice
Spices and seasoning
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the chicken in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 5 minutes until browned.

  3. 3

    Stir in the chopped tomatoes and chilli flakes, and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then stir in the peanut butter until smooth, simmer for 3 minutes, and serve over the rice.

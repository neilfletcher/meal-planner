Harissa Chickpea & Sweet Potato Traybake

A single portion of Moroccan cooking, ready in 33 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
25 min
Total
33 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 200gsweet potato, cubed
  • ½red onion, cut into wedges
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 200gtinned chickpeas, drained
  • 1 ½ tbspharissa paste
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Toss the sweet potato with the oil and half the harissa on a baking tray, and roast for 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Add the chickpeas and red onion, tossed through the remaining harissa, and roast for a further 10 minutes until the sweet potato is tender and caramelised at the edges.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Scatter with coriander to serve.

Beef Bulgogi Lettuce Cups

A single portion of Korean cooking, ready in 14 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
8 min
Total
14 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef frying strips
Fresh produce
  • 1little gem lettuce
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 3 tbspshop-bought bulgogi marinade
  • ½ tspsesame seeds
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the beef through the bulgogi marinade while you prepare everything else.

  2. 2

    Separate the lettuce into whole leaves and arrange on a plate.

  3. 3

    Heat a frying pan or wok over high heat and stir-fry the beef and its marinade for 2–3 minutes until browned and sticky.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Spoon the beef into the lettuce leaves, scatter with sliced spring onion and sesame seeds, and eat wrapped like a taco.

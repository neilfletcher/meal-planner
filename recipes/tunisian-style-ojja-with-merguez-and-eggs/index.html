Tunisian-Style Ojja with Merguez and Eggs

A single portion of Tunisian cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
18 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2merguez sausages (about 120g)
Fresh produce
  • ½red pepper, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 2eggs
Store cupboard
  • 200gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tspharissa paste
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
Bakery
  • 1crusty bread roll
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the sliced merguez in a pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.

  2. 2

    In the same pan, soften the pepper, onion and garlic for 4–5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tomatoes, harissa and cumin, return the sausage to the pan, and simmer for 8–10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Make two wells in the sauce and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook for 4–5 minutes until the eggs are just set.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then scatter with coriander and serve with crusty bread.

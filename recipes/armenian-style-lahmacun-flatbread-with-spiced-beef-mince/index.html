Armenian-Style Lahmacun Flatbread with Spiced Beef Mince

A single portion of Armenian cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
9 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, finely chopped
  • ½red pepper, finely chopped
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
  • 1lemon wedge
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbsptomato puree
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ½ tsppaprika
  • pinchchilli flakes
Bakery
  • 1large flatbread or pitta
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 220C (fan 200C).

  2. 2

    Mix the onion, pepper and garlic with the beef mince, tomato puree, cumin, paprika, chilli flakes and half the parsley. Season well.

  3. 3

    Spread the mince mixture thinly and evenly over the flatbread, right to the edges.

  4. 4

    Bake on a tray for 8–9 minutes until the meat is cooked and the edges are crisp.

  5. 5

    Scatter with the remaining parsley, squeeze over lemon and roll or fold to eat.

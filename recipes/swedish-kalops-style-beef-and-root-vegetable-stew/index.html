Swedish Kalops-Style Beef and Root Vegetable Stew

A single portion of Swedish cooking, ready in 31 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
22 min
Total
31 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gbeef rump steak, cut into 2cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1carrot, sliced
  • 150gpotatoes, halved
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspbutter
Store cupboard
  • 200mlbeef stock
  • 1 tspplain flour
  • 2 tbsppickled beetroot
Spices and seasoning
  • 4whole allspice berries
  • 1bay leaf
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15–18 minutes until tender.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, melt the butter in a pan over high heat and sear the beef for 1–2 minutes until browned. Tip onto a plate.

  3. 3

    Soften the onion and carrot in the same pan for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Stir the flour into the vegetables, then gradually add the stock, stirring to avoid lumps. Add the allspice and bay leaf and simmer for 10 minutes until the carrot is tender.

  5. 5

    Return the beef and its juices for 1–2 minutes, then remove the bay leaf.

  6. 6

    Serve with the potatoes and pickled beetroot.

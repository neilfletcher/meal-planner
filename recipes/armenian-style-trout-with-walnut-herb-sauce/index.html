Armenian-Style Trout with Walnut Herb Sauce

A single portion of Armenian cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1trout fillet, about 180g
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1 handfulfresh coriander or parsley
Store cupboard
  • 30gwalnuts
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbspplain flour
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 pinchchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Blitz or finely chop the walnuts, garlic and herbs together with the vinegar, chilli flakes and 1 tbsp of the olive oil to make a chunky walnut sauce. Season to taste.

  2. 2

    Dust the trout fillet lightly in flour.

  3. 3

    Heat the remaining oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the trout, skin-side down, for 3–4 minutes, then flip and cook for 2 minutes more until just cooked through.

  4. 4

    Spoon the walnut herb sauce over the trout to serve.

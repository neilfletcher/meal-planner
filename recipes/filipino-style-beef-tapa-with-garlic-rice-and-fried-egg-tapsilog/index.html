Filipino-Style Beef Tapa with Garlic Rice and Fried Egg (Tapsilog)

A single portion of Filipino cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef sirloin, thinly sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1 tbsplime juice
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1garlic clove, extra, finely chopped, for the rice
  • 1tomato, sliced, to serve
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tspsugar
  • 150gcooked rice
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Marinate the beef in the soy sauce, lime juice, garlic and sugar for 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Heat half the oil in a pan and fry the extra garlic until golden, then stir through the rice until fragrant. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Wipe the pan, heat the rest of the oil until very hot, and fry the beef for 1–2 minutes each side until well browned.

  4. 4

    In the same pan, fry the egg to your liking.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then plate the garlic rice, beef and fried egg together with the sliced tomato.

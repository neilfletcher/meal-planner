Ghanaian-Style Kontomire Stew with Smoked Mackerel

A single portion of Ghanaian cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 100gsmoked mackerel fillet, flaked
Fresh produce
  • 150gspinach, chopped
  • ½onion, chopped
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • ½stock cube
Spices and seasoning
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the onion, garlic and chilli flakes for 2–3 minutes until softened.

  2. 2

    Add the chopped tomato and cook for 2 minutes until pulpy.

  3. 3

    Crumble in the stock cube with a splash of water, then stir in the spinach and cook for 3–4 minutes until wilted.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Fold through the flaked smoked mackerel and warm through for 2 minutes. Serve hot, with rice if you like.

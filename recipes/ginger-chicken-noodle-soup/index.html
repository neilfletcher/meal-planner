Ginger Chicken Noodle Soup

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1thumb-sized piece fresh ginger, finely sliced
  • 1garlic clove, finely sliced
  • handfulpak choi or spinach
  • 1spring onion, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 400mlchicken stock
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 60gegg noodles
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Finely slice the ginger and garlic.

  2. 2

    Bring the chicken stock to a simmer in a saucepan with the ginger, garlic and soy sauce.

  3. 3

    Add the sliced chicken and simmer for 5–6 minutes until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Add the noodles and pak choi, and cook for a further 3–4 minutes until the noodles are tender.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Ladle into a bowl and scatter with sliced spring onion.

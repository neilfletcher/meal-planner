Russian-Style Beef Stroganoff

A single portion of Russian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef sirloin, cut into strips
Fresh produce
  • 80gmushrooms, sliced
  • ½onion, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 100mlbeef stock
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 80gtagliatelle
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tsppaprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the tagliatelle according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Sear the beef strips in a hot pan for 2 minutes, then remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Fry the onion and mushrooms in the same pan for 4–5 minutes until golden.

  4. 4

    Add the garlic, paprika and stock, and simmer for 3–4 minutes.

  5. 5

    Stir in the soured cream and mustard, return the beef and warm through for 2 minutes without boiling.

  6. 6

    Season to taste, then scatter with parsley and serve with the tagliatelle.

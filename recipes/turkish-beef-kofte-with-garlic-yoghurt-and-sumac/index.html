Turkish Beef Köfte with Garlic Yoghurt and Sumac

A single portion of Turkish cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
12 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, grated
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbspfresh parsley, chopped
  • ½garlic clove, crushed, extra
Dairy and eggs
  • 100gnatural yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ½ tspground cinnamon
  • ½ tspsumac
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the beef mince with the grated onion, garlic, cumin, cinnamon and parsley, and shape into 4–5 oval köfte.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the köfte for 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and cooked through.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile mix the yoghurt with the extra garlic and a pinch of salt.

  4. 4

    Warm the flatbread.

  5. 5

    Serve the köfte on the flatbread with the garlic yoghurt, dusted with sumac.

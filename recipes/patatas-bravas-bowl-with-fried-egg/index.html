Patatas Bravas Bowl with Fried Egg

A single portion of Spanish cooking, ready in 26 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
6 min
Cook
20 min
Total
26 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 200gpotato, cubed
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 100gchopped tomatoes
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the potato with the oil on a baking tray and roast at 200°C (fan 180°C) for 20 minutes until crisp, turning halfway.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, simmer the chopped tomatoes with the smoked paprika and chilli flakes for 8–10 minutes until thickened.

  3. 3

    Fry the egg to your liking.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the potatoes with the spiced tomato sauce spooned over and the fried egg on top.

Hawaiian-Style Loco Moco

A single portion of Hawaiian cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • 50gmushrooms, sliced
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 10gbutter
Store cupboard
  • 60gwhite rice
  • 100mlbeef stock
  • 1 tspsoy sauce
  • 1 tspcornflour
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Shape the beef mince into a patty, season and fry for 4–5 minutes each side until cooked through, then keep warm.

  3. 3

    In the same pan, fry the mushrooms in the butter for 2–3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the stock and soy sauce, thicken with a cornflour and water slurry, and simmer for 2–3 minutes into a gravy.

  5. 5

    Fry the egg sunny-side up in a separate pan.

  6. 6

    Serve the patty over the rice, topped with the fried egg and gravy.

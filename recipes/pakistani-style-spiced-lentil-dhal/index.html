Pakistani-Style Spiced Lentil Dhal

A single portion of Pakistani cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve. It brings some heat.

Prep
6 min
Cook
15 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
VeganSpicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1garlic clove, finely chopped
Store cupboard
  • 80gdried red lentils
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ½ tspground turmeric
  • ¼ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Rinse the lentils, then simmer with the turmeric and 300ml water for 12 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, fry the onion and garlic in a splash of oil for 5 minutes until golden.

  3. 3

    Stir the cumin and chilli flakes into the onions, then stir this through the cooked lentils.

  4. 4

    Season and serve.

Chilean-Style Fish with Tomato Salsa

A single portion of Chilean cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gwhite fish fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1tomato, diced
  • ½red onion, diced
  • ½lime
Store cupboard
  • 70grice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Mix the tomato, onion and lime juice into a fresh salsa.

  3. 3

    Fry the fish in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes each side until just cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the fish over the rice, topped with the salsa.

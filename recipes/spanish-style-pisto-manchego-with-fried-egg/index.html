Spanish-Style Pisto Manchego with Fried Egg

A single portion of Spanish cooking, ready in 27 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
18 min
Total
27 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 1courgette, diced
  • ½red pepper, diced
  • ½green pepper, diced
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 200gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Bakery
  • 1 slicecrusty bread, to serve
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the olive oil in a pan and fry the onion, peppers and garlic for 4–5 minutes until softened.

  2. 2

    Add the courgette and cook for a further 3–4 minutes.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tinned tomatoes and smoked paprika, season, and simmer for 12–15 minutes until thick and jammy.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, fry the egg in a little oil until the white is set.

  5. 5

    Spoon the pisto into a bowl, top with the fried egg and serve with crusty bread.

Hungarian Stuffed Peppers with Pork and Rice (Töltött Paprika)

A single portion of Hungarian cooking, ready in 49 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
40 min
Total
49 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gpork mince
Fresh produce
  • 1large bell pepper, top cut off and deseeded
  • ¼onion, finely diced
  • 1garlic clove, minced
  • 1 handfulfresh parsley, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 40gcooked rice
  • 200gpassata
  • 1 tsptomato purée
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspsweet paprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan).

  2. 2

    Mix the pork mince, cooked rice, onion, garlic and paprika together and season well.

  3. 3

    Stuff the mixture into the hollowed pepper and stand it upright in a small ovenproof dish.

  4. 4

    Mix the passata with the tomato purée and pour around the pepper.

  5. 5

    Cover with foil and bake for 35–40 minutes until the pepper is tender and the filling is piping hot and cooked through.

  6. 6

    Serve with the tomato sauce spooned over, a dollop of soured cream and a scatter of parsley.

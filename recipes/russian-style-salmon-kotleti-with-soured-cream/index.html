Russian-Style Salmon Kotleti with Soured Cream

A single portion of Russian cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gsalmon fillet, finely chopped
Fresh produce
  • ¼onion, finely grated
  • 1 tspdill, chopped
  • 1lemon wedge
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
  • 15gbutter, for frying
  • 2 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 20gbreadcrumbs
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Finely chop the salmon to a coarse mince using a knife.

  2. 2

    Mix with the breadcrumbs, egg, grated onion, dill and seasoning.

  3. 3

    Shape into two patties.

  4. 4

    Fry in the butter for 3–4 minutes each side until golden and cooked through.

  5. 5

    Serve with a dollop of soured cream and a lemon wedge.

Chicken Karaage with Sesame Slaw

A single portion of Japanese cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gchicken thigh fillets, diced
Fresh produce
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1garlic clove, grated
  • 80gcabbage, shredded
  • 1lemon wedge
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 2 tbsppotato starch or cornflour
  • 100mlvegetable oil, for frying
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsesame seeds
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Marinate the chicken in the soy sauce, ginger and garlic for 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a small saucepan or deep frying pan over medium-high heat.

  3. 3

    Coat the marinated chicken pieces in the potato starch, shaking off any excess.

  4. 4

    Fry in batches for 6–7 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through, then drain on kitchen paper.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then serve over the shredded cabbage, scattered with sesame seeds and a lemon wedge.

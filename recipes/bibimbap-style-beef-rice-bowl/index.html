Bibimbap-Style Beef Rice Bowl

A single portion of Korean cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gbeef strips or mince
Fresh produce
  • 60gcarrot, julienned
  • 60gspinach
Dairy and eggs
  • 1egg
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tspsesame oil
  • 70gjasmine or short-grain rice
  • 1 tbspgochujang
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the beef with the soy sauce and sesame oil in a hot pan for 3–4 minutes until browned, then set aside.

  3. 3

    Quickly stir-fry the carrot for 2 minutes, then wilt the spinach in the same pan for 1 minute. Season each lightly.

  4. 4

    Fry the egg in a little oil, keeping the yolk runny.

  5. 5

    Build the bowl with the rice, beef and vegetables arranged separately, topped with the fried egg and a spoonful of gochujang.

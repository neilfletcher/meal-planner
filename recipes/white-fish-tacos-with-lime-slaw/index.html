White Fish Tacos with Lime Slaw

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gwhite fish fillet (pollock or cod)
Fresh produce
  • 60gred cabbage, shredded
  • ½lime
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 1 tbspsoured cream
Store cupboard
  • 1 tspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground cumin
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
Bakery
  • 2soft tortillas
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the fish in the cumin, paprika and a little salt.

  2. 2

    Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the fish for 3–4 minutes each side until cooked through and flaking easily. Break into large chunks.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, toss the shredded cabbage with a squeeze of lime juice.

  4. 4

    Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry pan or microwave.

  5. 5

    Fill the tortillas with the fish, lime slaw, a spoonful of soured cream and chopped coriander.

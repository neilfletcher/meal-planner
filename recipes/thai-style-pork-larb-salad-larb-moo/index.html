Thai-Style Pork Larb Salad (Larb Moo)

A single portion of Thai cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
8 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gpork mince
Fresh produce
  • 1shallot, thinly sliced
  • 1lime, juiced
  • 1 tbspmint, chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
  • 4lettuce leaves
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspfish sauce
  • 1 tbsptoasted rice powder (or crushed toasted rice)
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Dry-fry the pork mince in a hot pan for 6–7 minutes, breaking it up, until cooked through and lightly browned.

  2. 2

    Take off the heat and stir in the shallot, chilli flakes, fish sauce and lime juice.

  3. 3

    Fold through the toasted rice powder, mint and coriander.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Spoon into lettuce leaves to serve.

Char Siu-Style Pork Steaks with Steamed Rice

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 16 minutes with 6 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
6 min
Cook
10 min
Total
16 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork loin steak
Store cupboard
  • 2 tbsphoisin sauce
  • 1 tspsoy sauce
  • 1 tsphoney
  • 150gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspChinese five spice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the hoisin, soy sauce, honey and five spice, and coat the pork all over.

  2. 2

    Heat a frying pan or griddle over a medium heat (the sugary glaze burns on high) and cook the pork for 4–5 minutes each side, basting with any extra marinade, until glazed and cooked through.

  3. 3

    Season to taste, then rest for 2 minutes, then slice and serve with the rice.

Lebanese-Style Fattoush Salad with Grilled Halloumi

A single portion of Lebanese cooking, ready in 17 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
9 min
Cook
8 min
Total
17 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 2 handfulsmixed salad leaves
  • 1tomato, chopped
  • ½cucumber, chopped
  • 2radishes, sliced
  • 1spring onion, sliced
  • 1lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbspmint, chopped
Dairy and eggs
  • 100ghalloumi, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tsppomegranate molasses
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspsumac
Bakery
  • 1pitta bread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toast or fry the pitta until crisp, then tear into pieces.

  2. 2

    Fry the halloumi slices in a dry pan for 2 minutes each side until golden.

  3. 3

    Toss the salad leaves, tomato, cucumber, radish and spring onion with the mint.

  4. 4

    Whisk the oil, lemon juice, sumac and pomegranate molasses for the dressing, and toss through the salad.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then top with the halloumi and pitta chips to serve.

Pork Carnitas Bowl with Pineapple Salsa

A single portion of Mexican cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gpork loin steak, cut into 1.5cm pieces
Fresh produce
  • 1orange, juiced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • ½red onion, finely diced
  • 1 tbspfresh coriander, chopped
  • 1lime
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
  • 80gtinned pineapple chunks, drained and chopped
  • 100gcooked rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ½ tspdried oregano
  • pinchchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the pork with the cumin, oregano, garlic and orange juice.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a pan and fry the pork over high heat for 6–8 minutes, turning occasionally, until crisp at the edges and cooked through. Roughly chop.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile mix the pineapple, red onion, coriander, a squeeze of lime juice and chilli flakes for the salsa.

  4. 4

    Warm the rice.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the shredded pork over the rice, topped with the pineapple salsa and remaining lime wedges.

Spiced Cauliflower & Chickpea Traybake

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 33 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegan, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
25 min
Total
33 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Vegan

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • ½small cauliflower
  • ¼lemon
  • small handfulfresh coriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 150gchickpeas, drained
  • 1 ½ tbspolive oil
  • 1 tbsptahini
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ½ tspground turmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).

  2. 2

    Cut the cauliflower into florets and toss with the chickpeas, cumin, turmeric and olive oil on a baking tray.

  3. 3

    Roast for 22–25 minutes, turning halfway, until the cauliflower is tender and lightly charred.

  4. 4

    Whisk the tahini with a squeeze of lemon and a splash of water to make a drizzle.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve the roasted cauliflower and chickpeas drizzled with the tahini and scattered with coriander.

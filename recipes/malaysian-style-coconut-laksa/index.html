Malaysian-Style Coconut Laksa

A single portion of Malaysian cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • 60gbeansprouts
Store cupboard
  • 70grice noodles
  • 150mlcoconut milk
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspcurry powder
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Soak the rice noodles in boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2

    Fry the chicken with the curry powder and chilli flakes in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes.

  3. 3

    Pour in the coconut milk and 100ml water, and simmer for 3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Stir in the noodles and beansprouts, warm through and serve.

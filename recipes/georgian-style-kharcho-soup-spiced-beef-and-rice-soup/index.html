Georgian-Style Kharcho Soup (Spiced Beef & Rice Soup)

A single portion of Georgian cooking, ready in 33 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
25 min
Total
33 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gbeef mince
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1garlic clove, chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 40grice
  • 150gtinned chopped tomatoes
  • 20gwalnuts, crushed
  • 400mlbeef stock
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground coriander
  • ½ tspdried thyme
  • ½ tspchilli flakes
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Brown the mince in a pan over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up as it cooks.

  2. 2

    Add the onion and garlic and soften for 3–4 minutes.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tomatoes, stock, ground coriander, thyme and chilli flakes, and simmer for 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the rice and simmer for a further 12–15 minutes until the rice is tender.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Stir in the crushed walnuts and finish with fresh coriander.

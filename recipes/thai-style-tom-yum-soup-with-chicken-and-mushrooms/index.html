Thai-Style Tom Yum Soup with Chicken and Mushrooms

A single portion of Thai cooking, ready in 20 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
8 min
Cook
12 min
Total
20 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 120gchicken breast, sliced
Fresh produce
  • 1lemongrass stalk, bruised
  • 2kaffir lime leaves
  • 2slices fresh ginger
  • 80gchestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • 1red chilli, sliced
  • 1lime, juiced
  • 50gcherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 400mlchicken stock
  • 1 tbspfish sauce
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Bring the stock to a simmer with the lemongrass, lime leaves and ginger, and simmer for 5 minutes to infuse.

  2. 2

    Add the chicken, mushrooms and cherry tomatoes, and simmer for 6–8 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  3. 3

    Stir in the fish sauce, lime juice and chilli, and taste, adjusting the balance of sour, salty and hot.

  4. 4

    Ladle into a bowl and scatter with coriander to serve.

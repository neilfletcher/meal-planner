Kenyan-Style Grilled Spiced Lamb (Nyama Choma-Inspired)

A single portion of Kenyan cooking, ready in 18 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
10 min
Total
18 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180glamb leg steak or chops
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 1lemon, juiced
  • 1tomato, diced
  • ¼red onion, diced
  • 1red chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tbspcoriander, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground coriander
  • 1 tsppaprika
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Mix the garlic, ginger, ground coriander, paprika, half the lemon juice and the oil into a paste and rub over the lamb.

  2. 2

    Marinate for 5 minutes, then grill or fry over high heat for 4–5 minutes each side until charred and cooked to your liking. Rest for 2 minutes.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, toss the tomato, red onion, chilli, coriander and remaining lemon juice for a kachumbari-style salad.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the lamb with the salad.

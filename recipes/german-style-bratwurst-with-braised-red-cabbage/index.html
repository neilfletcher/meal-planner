German-Style Bratwurst with Braised Red Cabbage

A single portion of German cooking, ready in 21 minutes with 7 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
7 min
Cook
14 min
Total
21 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 2bratwurst sausages
Fresh produce
  • 200gred cabbage, shredded
  • ½apple, sliced
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Fry the cabbage and apple in a splash of oil over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.

  2. 2

    Stir in the vinegar and cook for 2 minutes more.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, fry or grill the sausages for 10–12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  5. 5

    Serve the sausages with the braised cabbage and mustard.

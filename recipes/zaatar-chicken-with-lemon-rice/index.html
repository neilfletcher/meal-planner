Za'atar Chicken with Lemon Rice

A single portion of Lebanese cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 180gchicken thigh, sliced
Fresh produce
  • ½lemon
  • small handfulfresh parsley, chopped
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 70gbasmati rice
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 ½ tbspza'atar
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions, then stir through the lemon zest, a squeeze of juice and the chopped parsley.

  2. 2

    Toss the chicken with the za'atar and olive oil.

  3. 3

    Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the chicken for 6–7 minutes, turning occasionally, until cooked through.

  4. 4

    Season to taste, then serve the chicken over the lemon rice.

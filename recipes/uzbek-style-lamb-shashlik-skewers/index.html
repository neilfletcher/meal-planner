Uzbek-Style Lamb Shashlik Skewers

A single portion of Uzbek cooking, ready in 19 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
9 min
Cook
10 min
Total
19 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150glamb leg steak, cut into 2.5cm cubes
Fresh produce
  • ½onion, grated
  • ¼onion, thinly sliced, for serving
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • 1 tspcoriander seeds, crushed
  • ½ tsppaprika
  • ½ tspsumac
Bakery
  • 1flatbread
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Toss the lamb cubes with the grated onion, cumin, coriander seeds, paprika, oil and salt.

  2. 2

    Leave to marinate briefly while the grill or a griddle pan heats.

  3. 3

    Thread the lamb onto skewers and grill or griddle for 8–10 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked to preference.

  4. 4

    Toss the sliced onion with the sumac.

  5. 5

    Serve the skewers with warm flatbread and the sumac onion.

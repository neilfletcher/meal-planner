Shandong-Style Braised Fish in Brown Sauce

A single portion of Chinese cooking, ready in 23 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Total
23 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 1white fish fillet, about 180g (e.g. seabass or cod)
Fresh produce
  • 1garlic clove, sliced
  • 1 thumbfresh ginger, sliced
  • 2spring onions, cut into batons
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbspcornflour
  • 2 tbspvegetable oil
  • 1 tbsplight soy sauce
  • 1 tbspdark soy sauce
  • 1 tspChinese black vinegar
  • 1 tspsugar
  • 100mlchicken or vegetable stock
  • 150gcooked rice
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Pat the fish dry and dust lightly all over with cornflour.

  2. 2

    Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and fry the fish for 2–3 minutes each side until golden and just cooked. Remove and set aside.

  3. 3

    Add the garlic, ginger and half the spring onion to the pan and stir-fry for 30 seconds.

  4. 4

    Add the soy sauces, vinegar, sugar and stock, bring to a simmer and cook for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.

  5. 5

    Return the fish to the pan, spoon the sauce over and simmer for 1–2 minutes to heat through.

  6. 6

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  7. 7

    Scatter with the remaining spring onion and serve with rice.

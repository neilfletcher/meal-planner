Malaysian-Style Assam Fish Curry

A single portion of Malaysian cooking, ready in 24 minutes with 9 minutes of prep. Written for one, so there is nothing to halve and nothing to waste. It brings some heat.

Prep
9 min
Cook
15 min
Total
24 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Spicy

Ingredients

Serves 1
Meat and fish
  • 150gwhite fish fillet
Fresh produce
  • 1shallot, sliced
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
  • 80ggreen beans, trimmed
Store cupboard
  • 1 tbsptamarind paste
  • 1 tspsambal oelek or chilli paste
  • 1 tsplemongrass paste
  • 150mlfish or vegetable stock
  • 60gwhite rice
Spices and seasoning
  • ½ tspground turmeric
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the shallot, garlic, sambal and lemongrass paste for 2–3 minutes until fragrant.

  3. 3

    Stir in the tamarind paste, turmeric and stock, and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Add the fish and green beans, and simmer gently for 6–8 minutes until the fish is just cooked through.

  5. 5

    Taste and adjust the seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve with the rice.

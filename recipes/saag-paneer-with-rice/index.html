Saag Paneer with Rice

A single portion of Indian cooking, ready in 22 minutes with 8 minutes of prep. Vegetarian, and written for one, so there is nothing to halve.

Prep
8 min
Cook
14 min
Total
22 min
Serves
1
Add to my weekStart cooking
Veg

Ingredients

Serves 1
Fresh produce
  • 150gbaby spinach
  • 1garlic clove, crushed
Dairy and eggs
  • 150gpaneer, cubed
  • 2 tbspsingle cream or plain yoghurt
Store cupboard
  • 70gbasmati rice
  • 1 tbspvegetable oil
Spices and seasoning
  • 1 tbspmedium curry powder
Add to my shopping list

Method

  1. 1

    Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.

  2. 2

    Fry the paneer in the oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes until golden, then set aside.

  3. 3

    Fry the crushed garlic and curry powder in the same pan for 1 minute, then add the spinach and cook until wilted.

  4. 4

    Blend or roughly mash the spinach mixture, stir in the cream, then return the paneer to the pan and warm through.

  5. 5

    Season to taste, then serve over the rice.
